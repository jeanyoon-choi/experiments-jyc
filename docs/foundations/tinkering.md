# Tinkering

Working method for the experimental archives in `apps/scc`, `apps/goldfishes`,
`apps/six-sigma`, and `apps/xyt-cube`.
C-VAL and ddong-meong are finished projects; apply experiments there only when requested.

Learn through bounded interventions in working material. Taleb's convex tinkering
suggests limited downside with room for unexpected discoveries. Paik's altered
televisions, signal mixing, and collaborations, and Rauschenberg's combinations
of ordinary materials, performance, art, and engineering are precedents for
altering an instrument and learning through making. This is our working
translation, not their proposed software methodology or a historical surface
style to imitate.

## Working contract

- Start from the closest complete baseline. Keep a preserved result reachable
  before changing it — as the default of a new option on the same experiment
  (preferred in SCC; see [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md)) or as a fork; vary one
  coherent relation (or an inseparable set) and protect its
  visual/behavioral invariants.
- Keep the intervention local. Avoid dependencies, migrations, broad renames,
  and shared abstractions unless the question requires them. Standalone copies
  can preserve evidence: do not couple archived trials through shared imports.
- Finish a runnable, addressable proposition. Rapid iteration still requires
  resolved geometry, timing, material, composition, and interaction. Decorative
  reskins, unrelated feature piles, and unfinished components are not experiments.
- Judge the result against its baseline. Distinguish observed/measured effects
  from inference; report failed or uncertain results without inventing artistic
  intent, performance guarantees, or explanatory UI that excuses weak behavior.
- Keep useful surprises and failures. Further questions belong in notes or
  later forks, not an unrequested expansion of the current task. Promotion to
  `default` preserves the dated origin and its route.

Code, screens, networks, datasets, found media, browser behavior, and everyday
interfaces are artistic material. Technology must affect the tested relation:
a socket alone does not establish meaningful connection, nor 3D a meaningful
spatial experience. Authoring controls need not become participant UI. Follow
[design guidelines](design-guidelines.md) when creating or redesigning that UI.

## Durable record

In the owning experiment document, retain: route/date, tested relation, baseline,
changed variable, invariants, observed outcome (including failure/uncertainty),
and any unresolved question. Keep it short; never rewrite the archive as an
inevitable sequence of successes. Shared-file and parallel-work rules live in
[AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).
