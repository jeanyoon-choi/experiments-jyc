The first trial of moving from bubbles to 3D objects (user, 2026-10-25). It follows NOTES 1020's "It's still ontologically all bubbles, but shaped as 3d objects", in the line of Koons, the object, pop and the readymade.

Every page is still a bubble: its rank's area, its place in the raft, and the disc the goldfish reach and watch. It is only shown as an object. This is a test, expected to change and to move later into a 3D object lab of its own.

## The material is the bubbles'

The objects are drawn on the raft's canvas, after the raft, by the raft's own shading. The sphere shader's material and surroundings were moved into `SHADING` in `render/foam.ts`, unchanged, and the object pass (`objects/object-pass.ts`) uses that same code with each object's own normal in place of the sphere's. This covers:

- coat, metalness, roughness and clear coat;
- the photographed room, key light and ground;
- each page's own turn and hue.

So every look, coat and slider in 비주얼 applies to the objects exactly as to the bubbles; the default is magenta, as before. Not carried over: a sphere's mirrored neighbours and its seams, which belong to spheres pressed together.

The composite now writes each sphere's height as depth, so objects and any spheres still drawn pass in front of and behind one another.

**How each is built.**

- Inflated objects (`objects/shapes.ts`): a flat outline, every direction from its centre running out to the edge, with the height falling as a sphere's does over its disc.
- Hard-surface objects: `objects/models.ts` and `objects/gpu.ts`, from three.js primitives (used only to build the meshes).

**Fit to the disc.** Each object is fitted so its furthest point reaches past the disc by a per-kind fill (1.05–1.4). This keeps a star or a card covering about as much screen as the bubble would; the first fit covered 33–66% of the disc and read thin (user: "볼륨감이 덜 느껴짐").

**Which object a page shows.** It is fixed per page (a hash of the page's number), among the kinds that are on, by their shares.

**Travelling portions.** The portions under way along the links (왔다갔다 하는 것) are small objects of their source page's kind by default; 버블 brings back bubble/4's spheres.

**Changes during the day.**

- The first GPU was an inflated slab with fans and read as goggles; the user found it "별로 hyperreal 하지 않음", so it became the hard-surface card.
- A robot vacuum read only as a disc and was replaced by the Roadster (user).
- A dilution refrigerator (the gold "chandelier" of stacked plates, rods and coils) was tried and taken out (user).
- The VR headset gained its strap, and became a three-quarter view, after it read as a tray.

## Cost

Measured with `?measure` (Brave, MacBook Air, 1,000 pages, 380 fish, every portion an object), over 8 s:

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Frame interval, median / 95th percentile | 16.7 / 17.4 ms (60 fps) |
| Raft GPU (spheres and objects) | 5.8 ms (bubble/4's spheres: 6.3–6.8) |
| Fish GPU | 2.1 ms |

**Draw calls.** One instanced draw per kind and level of detail. An object under 14 px in radius uses a lighter mesh (half the segments, the GPU without its fins and blades). Detailed meshes run 2–16k triangles (`objects/shapes.test.ts` builds every one).

**Removing it.** Delete `objects/` and the lines marked 오브제 in:

- `render/frame-loop.ts`;
- `index.tsx`;
- `settings.ts`;
- `panels/panel-triggers.tsx` (the 오브제 trigger).

`SHADING` and the composite's depth can stay.
