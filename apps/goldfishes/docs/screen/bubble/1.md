## 결 다양성 (2026-10-20)

Every bubble's film came from one recipe: the seed only shifted where the noise was read, and the pattern scaled with the radius, so the raft read as copies of one bubble (measured at 기본값 3 over 1000 bubbles: the spread of mean brightness between bubbles, 0.006–0.014, was below the contrast inside one, 0.020). A new visual slider, 결 다양성 (`textureDiversity`, 0 = the old recipe exactly), gives each bubble its own character from its seed, every variation centred on the recipe so the raft keeps its look:

- age, the shared hidden variable: an older bubble has a thinner film, a thin cap reaching lower and calmer currents; a younger one is thicker, faintly banded along its drainage and livelier;
- eddy size partly in absolute terms (∝ r^0.3, clamped), so large bubbles hold more and finer eddies than small ones;
- drainage leaning up to about ±25° off the vertical;
- marginal regeneration: broken thinner/thicker patches along the walls;
- curvature (dome depth ×0.7–1.3) and a slight tilt, so highlights do not all sit in one place.

A first pass with convection vortices, ±80° drainage, black-film fronts and one-sided thinning made the whole raft darker and busier (mean 0.986 → 0.971, inner contrast +65%) and read as forced; it was cut back to the above. At 0.75 the inner contrast and fine detail stay at the recipe's (0.022, 0.032) while their spread between bubbles rises by about 35–60%; 60 fps unchanged on the MacBook Air at DPR 2. Measurements move with the living raft, so single comparisons are noisy.

## 막 흐름 (2026-10-20)

The films stood still: the only motion was 막 흐름 속도, which morphs the noise in place (boiling, not transport) and was 0 in 기본값 3. A new slider, 막 흐름 (배수·대류) (`filmFlow`, 0 = still as before, 기본값 3 = 1), carries each film's pattern along a velocity field: the curl of a stream function inside the bubble's unit disc, so the film neither gathers nor thins and nothing crosses the rim. Its parts are drainage (thick film sinking through the middle, thin film rising along the walls, two rolls), a slow circulation whose sense and strength are the bubble's own, and slowly changing eddies; small bubbles' films move faster for their size, younger ones (결 다양성's age) are livelier. The pattern is read upstream along the local velocity in two copies half a 2.5 s cycle apart, each faded out as it resets, with the blend restored to the pattern's contrast (flow-noise compensation); each bubble keeps its own cycle so the raft never pulses. The wall plumes drift with it.

Checked with the raft's geometry frozen from the console: at 0 the film is unchanged over seconds; at 1 it drifts and folds downward and round. 60 fps unchanged at DPR 2. Automated pixel diffs in the automation tab were unreliable (canvas reads came back stale), so the judgement is visual.
