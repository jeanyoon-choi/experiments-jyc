# bubble/2

- Route: `/screen/bubble/2`
- Date: 2026-10-20
- Code: `components/screen/bubble/2/`
- Baseline: [`bubble/1`](./1.md)'s raft, network, goldfish and sound, copied locally; the film and the media are not carried (see the cleanup below)

Every bubble is a lobe of Jeff Koons's *Balloon Dog*: mirror-polished stainless steel under a transparent colour coat. 비주얼 → 코팅 picks 마젠타 (the default), 옐로, 블루, 오렌지, 레드, 스틸 (bare steel) or 섞음 (each bubble one of the five coats).

What the material copies, from 27 photographs of the five dogs (e.g. Magenta at Versailles: [1](https://live.staticflickr.com/3056/3054111372_96cf7513b6_b.jpg), [2](https://live.staticflickr.com/3203/3075259150_6ef41d4862_b.jpg); Yellow on the Met roof: [3](https://live.staticflickr.com/3616/3469645017_860291f51c_b.jpg); Blue at LACMA and the Broad: [4](https://live.staticflickr.com/6077/6059855238_858dd091fa_b.jpg), [5](https://live.staticflickr.com/4159/33795582783_078a2df8df_b.jpg); Magenta in a white gallery: [6](https://live.staticflickr.com/3758/19635770999_16124e469c_b.jpg)) and, as counter-examples, a glazed ceramic imitation and a generic metal render:

- no diffuse light at all: the form is read only through the room it reflects; the coat tints that reflection, so bright things come back pale and tinted, dark things deep and dense;
- the room: a bright ceiling with converging light strips and lamps (small clipped highlights), white walls and tall windows broken by a dark band of visitors and frames — the horizon that bends round every lobe — and a pale floor brightening towards the plinth;
- each lobe turned and tipped a little (the dog's legs, body and ears point different ways), so horizons and windows never repeat; a crisp plinth band, physically right on a sphere, read as a row of smiles and was removed;
- round inflated lobes pinched into dark creases where they press against a neighbour (the twists and clefts), the steel there reflecting the neighbour's coat again;
- a clear top coat that reflects untinted at grazing angles, and a roll-off that takes highlights towards white.

The reference photographs were only studied, not added to the app. 60 fps at DPR 2 on the MacBook Air, as with the film.

## Rebuilt from the photographs (2026-10-20)

The first version reflected a crisp, graphic room — rows of windows, figures, ceiling strips, a plinth band — identically in every bubble: it read as a texture printed on balls, flat and cheap. Studied again at full resolution, the photographs show something else: most of each lobe is a smooth, deep gradient of its coat; the room is soft and barely legible; light arrives as long soft streaks (tall windows stretched along the curve) and a few tiny clipped points (lamps); and lobes mirror one another, which is why no two look alike.

Now:

- each pixel's reflected ray is traced against the neighbouring bubbles as spheres; a hit shows that lobe — its coat and its own reflection of the room — so every bubble's look comes from its own neighbourhood;
- the room is broad light only: bright above, dimmer at the horizon and in the room behind the camera, a pale floor brightening towards the plinth, soft window streaks, sparse tiny lamps, a faint soft darkening for visitors; it is read from the 3D direction, so there is no azimuth seam through the lobes' centres;
- each lobe is turned, tipped and rolled (as the dog's legs, tail and ears point different ways), so its horizon belt tilts and its streaks fall differently;
- lobes stay round to the edge and meet in a narrow dark cleft (a few pixels at any size; the earlier wide crease read as machined bevels on large bubbles), with occlusion there and the neighbour's reflection around it;
- a filmic (ACES) curve keeps dark reflections dense in the coat and rolls highlights to white.

Checked in the browser before and after, with large bubbles in view; 60 fps at DPR 2.

## Crisp gallery, shared surroundings, own controls (2026-10-20)

The soft room still read as plastic on large bubbles (an even wash of light, flat), and a clear coat at full Fresnel plus a 1.4 css px edge put a grey halo round every silhouette. Now:

- the gallery (창) is few, simple shapes with hard edges and a wide range: a dim room whose ceiling lightens upwards, a crisp horizon with the pale floor and plinth below, and three tall, very bright windows divided by glazing bars (as at Versailles), which the curve stretches into streaks, plus a few tiny lamps. A square light box and a ceiling strip were tried and dropped: small isolated shapes repeat on every lobe as symbols;
- the silhouette is anti-aliased over one device pixel and the clear coat is weak (클리어코트, default 0.25), so the edge stays deep in the coat's colour;
- 반사되는 환경 applies to the steel too: 창 is the gallery; 흐린 하늘, 스튜디오, 밤 도시, 나뭇잎 빛, 물빛 and an uploaded image are reflected as the film reflects them, over the room's dim light and the white floor (so a studio or a night city reads as their lights on deep coat, not black). 환경 선명도·대비 sets the reflection's sharpness, and 빛 its colour;
- Koons's own sliders: 빛 세기, 방 밝기, 바닥 반사, 클리어코트, 이웃 반사, 마디 방향 다양성, 주름 깊이, and a custom 코팅 colour; 쿤스 기본값으로 resets them. With 쿤스 the film's sliders, palettes, film colour and the 미디어 panel are hidden; 버블 면적, 흐름 버블, 노출 and 배경 stay.

The previous state is kept as a checkpoint outside the repository for a revert. Checked in the browser with large bubbles and every environment; 60 fps at DPR 2.

## Product-studio light instead of a reflected room (2026-10-20)

The crisp gallery (barred windows, horizon, floor line, lamps) was the wrong direction: the user had said the surroundings must not be visibly reflected, and the captures were worse, not better. Rules now: nothing nameable is reflected — no windows, horizon, floor line, lamps or texture; no small shape repeated on every lobe (a disc highlight, a square light box, a ceiling strip and a crisp black card round the lens all read as symbols or pupils and were removed); no high-frequency variation (noise patches read as a blotchy texture).

What is left is how chrome is lit in a product studio: a white sweep all round (the coat vivid over most of the steel), two large black flags at the sides that the mirror bends into crisp dark crescents round each rim, one graded softbox overhead, the camera's side a little and softly darker, and the soft bounce of the white floor. 반사되는 환경 changes these lights' placement, shape and colour (broad overhead light, two side strip boxes, small warm and cool city lights, greenish patches, a drifting cool strip), never shows a scene. Lobes still mirror their neighbours and meet in a dark cleft; edges are anti-aliased over one device pixel.

Open: the overhead softbox's highlight is similar on every lobe (orientation varies it); the centre of large lobes still falls off softly.

## Photographed light (2026-10-20)

The drawn studio lights still read as cheesy and plastic, and the overhead softbox left the same pill-shaped highlight on every lobe. The steel now reflects a real room's light: six CC0 HDRIs from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/hdris) (photo_studio_loft_hall for 창, kloofendal_overcast, studio_small_09, shanghai_bund, forest_slope, lakeside), each downsampled to 256 × 128, blurred past legibility, normalised to the same mean brightness and shipped as RGBA half floats in `public/assets/goldfishes/koons-light/` (256 KB each). Each lobe, turned its own way, catches its own part of the light, so no drawn shape repeats. Koons's sliders: 환경 밝기, 반사 흐림 (the light map's mip level), 환경 회전, 빛 대비, 코팅 농도, 어두운 곳 밝기, 아래 반사광, 클리어코트, 이웃 반사, 마디 방향 다양성, 주름 깊이, and the 코팅 colour.
