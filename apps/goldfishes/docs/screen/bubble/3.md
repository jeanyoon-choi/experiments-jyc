# bubble/3

- Route: `/screen/bubble/3`
- Date: 2026-10-22
- Code: `components/screen/bubble/3/`
- Baseline: [`bubble/2`](./2.md), copied locally (raft, network, goldfish, sound)

A lab for the spheres' material. bubble/2's steel reflected a room seen from the side: a white floor with window light from one side, an object on a table. The raft is the goldfish's tank, so here the spheres float in a white liquid, seen from straight above (the user's two reference images: crimson metallic spheres in milk).

## First version (2026-10-22): the light

"Up" is towards the camera, so the horizon runs round every sphere's rim. The outer band of each sphere mirrors the liquid around it, all the way round, tinted by the coat. Its middle mirrors the room overhead: dim, except for one key light that gives the raft its direction, and a weaker fill opposite.

- **Key:** a softbox, hotter at its far end and middle and never a flat plate, many times brighter than the room. Even the few per cent a clear coat reflects of it reads white, so the highlight is a white-hot core with a soft spill, as in the photographs, not a pink sticker.
- **Room:** brighter on the key's side (빛 방향성).
- **Photographed light (사진 빛):** the CC0 HDRIs shared with bubble/2 can be mixed into the room to break up the drawn light.
- **Liquid:** darker straight under each sphere, where it lies in the sphere's own shade.

## First version: the material

It runs from coloured metal (the coat tints every reflection, towards white at grazing angles) to glossy lacquer (the coat is the body's own colour, lit, under a clear gloss). A sharp clear coat sits over either. Each sphere may differ a little in roughness, coat density, hue and turn (구슬마다 차이). Reflected rays are traced against neighbouring spheres, so spheres mirror one another. Spheres sit with their waterline at or above their equator (잠김 깊이).

## First version: the liquid round the spheres (the separate low-resolution pass is kept)

The liquid climbs each sphere (a meniscus lit from the key's side), is shaded close in and cast away from the key, and picks up the sphere's colour.

These are drawn per sphere into two low-resolution targets: slopes and colours add, shade and nearness take the maximum. The composite reads them filtered. Computed in the composite from the nearest two spheres alone, they stepped with the texel grid wherever the nearest sphere changed.

The liquid's colour is 배경, and it is what the rims reflect. Faint ripples run across it, lit from the key's side.

## Parameters, reworked (2026-10-22)

The first set was too large (31 sliders) and its looks too alike: every look was a variation on the milk reference, and its contact shadow and rim shade were baked in. bubble/2's look had gone. User:

> 파라미터 셋들도 양만 존나 많지 … 다 사실 '연출'이 비슷비슷함 … 지금 이 버젼과, /2 버젼을 포괄한, 다양한 (또 다른) 연출들

The parameters are now those of a physically based material and its surroundings, the same quantities as three.js `MeshPhysicalMaterial` plus an environment map. A look therefore carries over if the spheres become 3D objects. The spheres themselves are not three.js: they are discs in a 2D layout, shaded per pixel in one GLSL composite, with reflected rays traced analytically against neighbouring spheres; three.js draws only the goldfish.

What the main sliders do:

- **시점** is the axis that contains both versions. At 0 the camera looks straight down, so the horizon runs round every rim and each sphere floats in a tank. At 1 the camera looks from the side, so the horizon crosses the spheres, as in bubble/2.
- **빛 방향** turns the photographed room and the key light together.
- **구슬마다 차이** turns each sphere its own way to the light, as bubble/2's lobes were turned, so reflections are not the same on every sphere. It also varies roughness and hue slightly.
- **가장자리 그림자 and 색 번짐** at 0 remove the shade on the ground, the dark rims and the coloured halo entirely. With both at 0 the ground pass is skipped and no halos are drawn.
- **주광** is a softbox far brighter than the room, rounded well past a square: a square read as the same sticker on every sphere.

연출, each a different environment, material and viewpoint:

- **갤러리 (bubble/2)** (the opening look): bubble/2's magenta steel in the photographed loft, from the side. Compared side by side with bubble/2 in the browser.
- **우유 수조:** crimson in milk from above, the references.
- **크롬:** bare chrome at a slant in the studio.
- **금빛 숲:** gold in a forest's light.

## Ground and reflections, corrected (2026-10-22)

**The ground round the spheres.** 우유 수조, 크롬 and 도자기 showed a white band and a grey ring round every sphere, which cannot happen to a sphere floating in milk seen from above. The cause was the meniscus lighting: the climbing liquid's slope was lit from the key's side. It is gone. What is left:

- **Shade:** the light a sphere hides from the ground, about (r / distance)⁴ and centred a little away from the key. A large sphere shades further, a bead barely at all, and there is no ring or band.
- **Colour bleed:** only right beside the sphere ((r / distance)¹⁰). A slower falloff spread into a pink haze round the whole raft.

**Reflections that differ realistically.** The user asked for reflections that differ a little from sphere to sphere. Random turns alone are not realistic, so the room is now finite:

- The key hangs at 0.9 × the screen's diagonal from the raft's middle, and the photographed room is a sphere of 1.5 × the diagonal round it.
- Each sphere sees both from where it is (parallax). Highlights and reflections shift across the raft as they do in a photograph.
- 구슬마다 차이 now only adds a slight turn (0.15 in the realistic looks; 갤러리 keeps bubble/2's full turn).

**환경 일론 머스크.** The portrait (`/images/0908/tech-power-faces/008.jpg`, Goldfishes' own tech-power faces) is the whole environment. Reflected directions map onto it as a mirror ball's map onto its disc (a sphere map), so every sphere reflects the face across its whole surface, compressed towards the rim.

A first try hung it as a print in the studio, but it filled only a small label in each sphere's middle, which is not what was asked.

## 갤러리 · 거칠기 1, and no 도자기 (2026-10-22)

The user's aim includes surfaces that show no reflected surroundings at all, so 갤러리 · 거칠기 1 is bubble/2's look at roughness 1 with no clear coat. A clear coat's sharper reflection would still show specks. 도자기 was removed at the user's request.

Two fixes made it clean, and both apply to every look:

- **Neighbour reflection:** it now fades with roughness above 0.3 (갤러리 at 0.29 is unaffected). A rough surface blurs its neighbours away with everything else; before, they stayed as crisp ellipses.
- **The blurred panorama's poles:** they ease into the room's average. Otherwise every azimuth meets in a pinched point, which showed on some spheres.


## JSON 내려받기 (2026-10-22)

Under 처음 값으로, JSON 내려받기 saves every panel's current values as `bubble-3-<time>.json`. The file holds:

- `look`: the visual panel's look, in the form a `LOOKS` entry takes.
- `raft`: area and portions.
- `network`: view, rates, damping, pages, groups and diversity.
- `fish`: count, parameters, palette and influence.
- `sound`.

A look the user likes is handed back as this file and becomes a 연출 button by pasting `look` into `LOOKS` in `settings.ts`.

## 웹에 주는 영향: the goldfish's feedback, made strong enough to matter (2026-10-22)

By default the school's watching raises a page's log quality by at most 0.13 per second, even with all 380 fish on one page. That is two orders below the quality's own noise (volatility 1.5; stationary SD 3.75), so the fish perform the rank rather than change it.

Three sliders in 금붕어 → 웹에 주는 영향 change this. Their defaults keep the earlier behaviour:

- **품질에 주는 영향:** 0–20 (was 0–1).
- **영향 상한:** 0–10. The most watching gives a page above its group mean; it was fixed at 1.5.
- **무리 크기 반영:** 0–1. At 0 one school's effect is shared among its fish, so more fish give the same total. At 1 every fish counts alone: 380 fish, about 13 times.

**Measured on the web model alone** (60 s, 8 seeds, 1,000 pages, volatility 1.5): ten fish watching one ordinary page continuously take its rank, as a multiple of the average page, from

| Setting | Rank (× average) |
|---|---|
| No fish | 4.3 |
| Default | 4.3 |
| 영향 20 | 5.3 |
| 영향 20, 상한 10 | 12.6 |
| All three at their maximum | 338, about a third of all rank |

In the running piece the fish spread over many pages and change page every half second, so the effect is shared out. The JSON export records these three values under `fish.feedback`.
