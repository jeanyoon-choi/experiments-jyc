# bubble/6

- Route: `/screen/bubble/6`
- Date: 2026-10-25
- Code: `components/screen/bubble/6/`
- Baseline: [`bubble/5`](./5.md), copied locally with its objects, looks, lifecycle and panels.

Every page as an object made one problem plain (user, 2026-10-25). The web's rich-get-richer took over within 20–30 s:

- a handful of objects covered the screen and the rest were specks;
- lowering 순위 쏠림 to almost nothing changed nothing.

The request: keep the motion (dynamic, churning, liquid, NOTES 1015–1020) and PageRank itself, but solve the gap between rich and poor at the level of the algorithm. Visual patches were ruled out, such as a cap on size or another parameter that shrinks bubbles. bubble/4 and /5 stay as they were.

## What was wrong (measured before the change)

**Recorded in the browser.** /4 and /5 were recorded from the start every 5 s for 70 s, with screenshots at 3, 13, 23, 33, 53 and 73 s. Both concentrated alike:

| | /4 (bubbles) | /5 (objects) |
|---|---|---|
| 5 s: top page / top tenth | 5.2% / 55% | 5.3% / 51% |
| From 15 s | 21–38% / 86% | 12–44% / 86% |

As bubbles this read well: a few large spheres pressed together and mirroring one another still form one foam. As objects it did not: at 40 times the median's width an icon is a poster, and the smallest ones are specks.

**Headless runs of the model** (800 pages, no fish) found the causes:

1. **Quality's spread grew with volatility.** Appeal is (PR + floor/N) · e^q, and log quality q drifted as an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck walk. At volatility 1.5 and reversion 0.08/s its stationary deviation is 1.5/√0.16 ≈ 3.75, reached about 12 s after the opening's 0.5, so appeals were thousands of times apart. The floor (순위 쏠림) cannot matter against that: Gini was 0.84 either way. Volatility 0.6 only got there later.
2. **New links are found in proportion to appeal.** With volatility 0, Gini still rose 0.40 → 0.58 over 90 s: preferential attachment.
3. **The goldfish feed back.** They watch the largest pages, which raises those pages' quality.
4. **Nothing pushes back.** A page that grew stayed grown until another grew larger.

## The change (web/model.ts): bubble/5's motion, the extreme bursting

**What stays bubble/5's, unchanged:** quality's wide Ornstein–Uhlenbeck swings, attention following appeal, link discovery, new pages, PageRank, the portions, the display (area = rank), the layout and the goldfish. The organic, evolving motion comes from these.

**One addition.** A page that grows past 터지는 크기 (`burstAt`, default 50) times its fair share 1/N tires. While it stays above, its fatigue F builds at 3 · ln(1 + N·PR − burstAt) per second without settling; its appeal is divided by e^F, so it always bursts. Below, F rests over 터진 뒤 쉬는 시간 (`recovery`, default 10 s). bubble/5's largest page reached some 1,600 times its share and covered the screen as an object.

**Measured in the browser** (from the start, real goldfish, `window.bubbleWebLog`; at 30–48 s):

| | bubble/5 | bubble/6 (burst 50) |
|---|---|---|
| Top page | 12–44% | 5–9% |
| Top tenth | 86% | 82–84% |
| Largest / median width | about 40× | 17–22× |
| Pages 2–5× the median's width ("middle") | about 10 (headless) | 45–60 |
| Pages ≥ 5× ("large") | about 10 (headless) | 31–48 |

So a few large, a few dozen middle and many small objects are visible at once: a graded hierarchy that keeps changing, instead of a few posters over specks. Headless (800 pages, no goldfish), the rank motion of the 50 largest is 0.44–0.49 per second against bubble/5's 0.30: the bursts add motion rather than remove it.

## How it got here (the same day)

1. **Bounded quality and soft fatigue** (spread 0.6, fatigue toward ln(1 + N·PR − 1)). Even (Gini 0.47, 3.5×), but the largest pages were shuffled every second or two: motion without meaning.
2. **Bounded quality, slow** (spread 0.3, about 22 s correlation), with fatigue that builds above 15× and only rests below, and 순위 쏠림 0.16. Hits held for 10–80 s, but the web barely moved. User: "거의 실패" — it had lost bubble/5's organic, evolving feel.
3. **Diagnosis:** that feel was bubble/5's quality swings; only the extreme they piled up was the problem. So quality went back to bubble/5's, and only the burst above a size was kept.
4. **Burst size.** 8 (largest 10–13×) and 30 (15–17×) left too little difference (user: "pure noise 처럼 정보량 없는"; "조금 더 빈부격차가 커야함"). It is now 50.

A gentler mapping from appeal to attention (attention ∝ appeal^β, β < 1) was simulated too. It thickened the middle, but slowed the motion below bubble/5's, so it was not used.

## Checking it

**In the browser, live.** In the 네트워크 panel, 관심 역학 switches between 기존 (bubble/5) and + 극단에서 터짐 on the same page. Its sliders are 터지는 크기 (2–100 times the share) and 터진 뒤 쉬는 시간 (1–40 s).

**`window.bubbleWebLog`** keeps, every 2 s:

- time and pages;
- the leader, the top share and the top tenth;
- Gini;
- the largest's width over the median's;
- the middle and large counts.

**`web/dynamics.test.ts`** runs both motions over 90 s: 800 pages, with a goldfish stand-in that raises the ten largest pages' quality each second. bubble/6 must:

- keep at least 60% of bubble/5's average spread;
- cut its peaks by at least 15%;
- move at least 90% as much.

## 각도 (objects)

In the 오브제 panel, 각도 has three settings:

- **똑바로:** every object upright, as before.
- **랜덤:** each its own fixed angle, from its seed.
- **움직이는 방향** (the default): each turned with its top towards where it is going. A portion faces along its link, bow included. A page faces the way it is pushed: its velocity is smoothed over 0.4 s and kept while it is still.

It costs one float per object; the frame cost is unchanged.
