## Failure: the first catalogue broke the object–material contract (2026-11-05)

The user, on seeing the first catalogue: the new objects were not covered by the material; some showed their own colours and lines, some only their lines took the material, and they had no volume. 통화 기호 and 유명인 얼굴 were right; the new ones were not. The lead's confession of what was broken, against bubble/5–8 (captured again in /7 and /8 on 2026-11-05: in /7 every object one chrome; in /8 every object one coat colour over its whole inflated body):

- **One material, whole.** bubble/5's rule ("one object … in one material"), kept through /8: choose 쿤스 (코팅) or any material and every object, whatever made it, takes that same material over all of it. The relief gave each layer its own paint and a separate albedo path (브랜드 색) for catalogue kinds, so a kind could carry its own colours and lines. Removed: catalogue kinds have no paint.
- **Volume.** bubble/5–7 inflate every flat sign (heart, logo, currency) as a Koons balloon over its outline (∇²u = −1, height 2√u). The relief was a flat slab with a bevel and engraved lines; on a flat face the reflective materials show one patch of the room (black app tiles), with the material only on the bevel and the lines. Now each layer is blown up as a balloon.
- **Not super-real.** 인프라·하부구조, 카지노·주화 and the hardware were CAD-accurate: every grille, vent, lattice, wire and line of lettering drawn. The objects of /5–8 are a little abstracted: chunky, rounded, the silhouette and the main masses only. All of 하드웨어·인프라, 인프라·하부구조 and 카지노·주화 were remodelled (user: "아주 약간 추상화"): rounded primitives, the silhouette and main masses only, every part at least ~0.06 of the disc across, no grilles, lettering or wire; the coins and chips keep one central motif. Checked in the raft under 맥시멀 믹스 and 쿤스 (코팅): one coat over every object, beside 통화 기호; 16.7 ms median, 0 frames over 20 ms. Weakest still: the wafer's die grid and the boards (H100, DDR5) read a little as pattern.
- **AI 아키텍처 and 공학·수학 기호: removed.** Line diagrams and notation have no volume (user: "완전히 볼륨감이 없음"). Signs without volume are not what this work shows.

## Failure: 달러·센트 (2026-11-05/06, removed)

The user asked for the real $1 bill and a real circulating cent. Made from the public-domain photographs (series 2009 note, 2010 cent): a soft cushion and a domed disc, each with its design turned into a height map over it (the note's ink darkness, the cent's high-passed luminance). The user: heavy texture, and still not read as a bill or a coin at all. Removed with its code (the relief `detail` map too).

Why it failed:
- **The identity was all on the surface.** A bill is a plain rectangle and a coin a plain disc; what makes them a dollar or a cent is the print and the die, which is exactly what the contract takes away (one material over everything, no own colours). 통화 기호, 하트 and the logos work because their silhouette is the sign.
- **A bill is known by colour and print, not relief.** Green-grey ink on cream paper, flat. Turned into relief under chrome or a coat, the print becomes a field of tiny bumps: texture, not Washington.
- **A photograph is not a height map.** The cent's luminance is light and shadow on its relief, not the relief; high-passing it gives edges and noise of the right size but not the forms, and the note's ink darkness is not relief at all.
- **Scale.** At raft size (tens of pixels) the detail that carried identity is below what one object shows; what remains is a rounded slab and a disc.

Rule kept (agent-onboarding, object party rules): a kind is only a candidate if its silhouette and volume alone name it.

## Morphs follow the goldfish, not a clock (2026-11-06)

Measured on bubble/7–9's morph code by simulation and 20–30 s captures: every page morphed on one global clock (변신 every 14 s, each on its hashed beat), so all melted and set alike, which read as stock computer animation; a portion set solid for 0.1–0.2 s mid-way between two quick morphs; a page retargeted before it had set dropped from melt 0.31 to 0 in one frame.

A first try added rules (a melt floor 잔류 유동, a 연속 액체 switch, a damped ring-down when setting): everything wobbled, nothing read, and every object moved the same way (user: worst). Removed. The rule now is fewer rules:
- **No clock.** 변신 간격 is gone. A page moves on to its next object once the goldfish have watched it for one fish-second (`TURN_WATCH`, frame-loop.ts `pageTurns`) and its last morph has set. Attention is uneven, so the watched pages flow from one object into the next while the unwatched stay what they are and are read — variety and solid anchors both come from the school, with no per-object settings.
- A retarget carries the melt on (no frame drops to solid).
- A portion is liquid all its way (one passage, the old two-leg one removed).
- **Tier sharpening** (`fields/tiers.ts`): bubble/9's fixed-memory tiers moved a large object's kind from a coarse copy to the 512² one when a slot freed; its outline went from soft to crisp in one frame, which read as liquid setting at once. A layer moved to a finer tier now starts as blurred as it was and sharpens over 0.6 s (two floats per object, no extra reads).
Frames: 16.7 ms median, p95 17.6, none over 20 ms (one tab, 600 frames). Files before the liquid work: scratchpad `before-liquid-flow/`.

The earlier way (clock, melt floor, ring-down) is kept as the second of `MORPH_STYLES` while the user decides; [`/screen/bubble/9b`](./9b.md) is this same code opening on it.
