## Failure: the first catalogue broke the object–material contract (2026-11-05)

The user, on seeing the first catalogue: the new objects were not covered by the material; some showed their own colours and lines, some only their lines took the material, and they had no volume. 통화 기호 and 유명인 얼굴 were right; the new ones were not. The lead's confession of what was broken, against bubble/5–8 (captured again in /7 and /8 on 2026-11-05: in /7 every object one chrome; in /8 every object one coat colour over its whole inflated body):

- **One material, whole.** bubble/5's rule ("one object … in one material"), kept through /8: choose 쿤스 (코팅) or any material and every object, whatever made it, takes that same material over all of it. The relief gave each layer its own paint and a separate albedo path (브랜드 색) for catalogue kinds, so a kind could carry its own colours and lines. Removed: catalogue kinds have no paint.
- **Volume.** bubble/5–7 inflate every flat sign (heart, logo, currency) as a Koons balloon over its outline (∇²u = −1, height 2√u). The relief was a flat slab with a bevel and engraved lines; on a flat face the reflective materials show one patch of the room (black app tiles), with the material only on the bevel and the lines. Now each layer is blown up as a balloon.
- **Not super-real.** 인프라·하부구조, 카지노·주화 and the hardware were CAD-accurate: every grille, vent, lattice, wire and line of lettering drawn. The objects of /5–8 are a little abstracted: chunky, rounded, the silhouette and the main masses only. All of 하드웨어·인프라, 인프라·하부구조 and 카지노·주화 were remodelled (user: "아주 약간 추상화"): rounded primitives, the silhouette and main masses only, every part at least ~0.06 of the disc across, no grilles, lettering or wire; the coins and chips keep one central motif. Checked in the raft under 맥시멀 믹스 and 쿤스 (코팅): one coat over every object, beside 통화 기호; 16.7 ms median, 0 frames over 20 ms. Weakest still: the wafer's die grid and the boards (H100, DDR5) read a little as pattern.
- **AI 아키텍처 and 공학·수학 기호: removed.** Line diagrams and notation have no volume (user: "완전히 볼륨감이 없음"). Signs without volume are not what this work shows.
- **Why it happened.** The lead wrote the catalogue contract (`types.ts`: "Not a balloon: faces are flat where the object's are") from what the real objects are, without reading /5–8's object–material decomposition as the law; the agents followed the contract and the lead passed contact sheets lit in plaster, never the raft in 맥시멀 믹스 and 쿤스, where the failure is plain.
