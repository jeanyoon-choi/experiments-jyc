## Framework

Raw WebGL2 with GLSL ES 3.00, drawn on the raft's own canvas:

- `render/foam.ts`: the raft's spheres and the shared material, `SHADING`.
- `objects/object-pass.ts`: the objects.
- three.js is used only to build geometry (`objects/models.ts`, `objects/gpu.ts`) and for the goldfish scene on its own canvas.
- React (Next.js) holds the panels and their state.

## Liquid morphing (`objects/fields.ts`, `objects/morph.ts`, `objects/object-pass.ts`)

**Why meshes were dropped.** Every kind had a mesh of its own, with nothing in common between them, so a mesh cannot flow into another.

**The field.** The raft is seen from above, so an object is only its top surface, a height over the plane. Each kind, and the bubble itself (layer 0), is baked once from its mesh into a 512² field over [−1.5, 1.5]²:

- the height of its top surface;
- the signed distance to its outline;
- the normal of its top surface.

Baking rasterises at 2× and uses an exact Euclidean distance transform. A 128² membrane field sits beside it: the Poisson balloon of `shapes.ts`, ∇²u = −1, height 2√u. Every kind takes 70–130 ms to bake.

**Baking off the main thread.** Baked one per frame in the loop, the fields stalled the first two seconds: fifteen frames of 100–167 ms, measured 2026-10-27. They are now baked by up to three workers (`objects/field-worker.ts`), and the bubble's field is written exactly, at once. The first seconds then showed three slow frames, which are the page's and WebGL's own start-up. Until its field arrives, a kind is drawn as a bubble.

**Drawing.** Every object is one square in one instanced draw, only as large as the outlines it flows between reach, plus room for the melt. Per pixel, the shader reads two kinds' fields from a texture array and blends them:

- **Outline.** The two signed distances interpolate, so the outline flows from one shape to the other.
- **Melted surface.** While melted, the surface is a drop held by surface tension: the two membranes blended, then rounded off at the flowing edge with a smooth minimum.
  - A drop built from the distance to the edge alone creased along the shape's middle (its medial axis). Seen in the 랩 on sparkle → heart; the membrane fixed it.
- **Melting washes out detail.** The fields' mip levels make fingers, fans and flaps dissolve into the drop.
- **Sway and ripples.** The outline sways slowly, and ripples run in from the edge.
- **Patches.** The object melts and sets in patches that move over it, rather than all at once. At first the next object's detail rose everywhere together, like a cross-fade.
- **수은화.** While melted, the coat turns to bare mirror, like the T-1000.
- **Depth.** The height is written as depth, as the meshes' was.

**Timing.** A morph runs 0 → 1 over its duration:

1. melts in about the first fifth;
2. flows as a spring, overshooting the new outline (말랑말랑) and settling before it sets;
3. sets over the last third.

**Where morphs happen.**

| What | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Pages | A page morphs whenever what it is shown as changes: the set's chips, 전파, or **변신**. With 변신, each page draws another object of its set about every `change` seconds (default 14 s, 0 off), on its own beat (NOTES 1020: "지속해서 바뀌고, 그 어디에도 멈춰있지 않은"). |
| Portions under way | Shape and morph are separate settings (user, 2026-10-27; the first version listed 「하트 → 도착 페이지 (모핑)」 among the shapes, which was wrong). 왔다갔다 하는 것 picks what a portion carries: 버블 / 페이지 따라 / 테크 하이프 / SNS. 모핑 is a plain 끔 / 켬 (true / false), on by default. On, it morphs a portion at both ends of its link, whatever it carries (`morph.ts` `passage`): it necks off as its source page's shape, melts into its own over the first 42% of the way, holds it across the middle, and melts into the shape of the page it reaches over the last 42%. |

**Settings.** The liquid's settings (`objects/liquid.ts`) are part of the objects' parameters, so the raft uses whatever is tuned in the 랩:

- duration
- melt
- softening
- wobble
- ripples
- overshoot
- drop height
- 수은화

**Cost.** Frame cost was measured with `?measure`, 800 pages, on this Mac:

| | Median / p95 frame | GPU |
|---|---|---|
| bubble/6 (meshes) | 18.4 / 19.0 ms | 9.7 ms |
| bubble/7 (one instanced draw of squares) | 14.7–15.2 / 16–20 ms | 5.8–6.1 ms |

`?measure` stalls the GPU on purpose to time it. Without it, frames run at the display's 60 Hz in all three modes: median 16.7 ms, p95 16.8–17.2 ms, measured over 8–10 s with no slow frames after start-up.

## 랩 (`objects/lab.ts`, `panels/mode-control.tsx`)

The 랩 shows one object in the middle of the screen, on the current 연출's room and material. The raft and the school are paused.

- **Picking a target.** A chip for every kind and for the bubble flows into it. Tapping the canvas moves on to the next in the sequence:
  - 하트 ↔ 각 오브제 (default): the user's "하트에서 각 오브제로";
  - 오브제 → 오브제;
  - 버블 ↔ 각 오브제.
- **자동 넘김** moves on by itself after 머무는 시간.
- **한 순간 멈춤** holds the morph at one share of its way, scrubbed by a slider, to study a single moment.
- Size and turn sliders, plus the liquid's sliders.
- Materials are tried through the visual panel, as in the raft.

## 전파 (`spread/contagion.ts`)

Requested by the user: "특정 추가 버블이 특정 컬러를 가지고 … 들어오면 그 컬러에 의해 차근차근히 전파되는 … 말 그대로 cas".

**Newcomers.** A newcomer is placed by hand (user, 2026-10-27: "색 선택 … 선택한 이후에, 화면 아무데나 클릭하면 그 오브제가 나오는거 … 그 색에 맞춰서 쭉 전파"):

1. Choose a colour: Squid Game's pink and teal, acid yellow, electric blue or orange. The chosen colour is ringed and raised.
2. Choose an object: any kind, or the bubble.
3. Tap anywhere: a new page appears there carrying both, linked from the page under the tap if there is one.

4. Or hold and draw (user, 2026-10-27: "finger skating 마냥 … 쭉 누르면서 위치 움직이면서 … 여러 오브제 쫘르르"): a newcomer every 30 px of the stroke, evenly along the coalesced pointer samples. Each newcomer is linked from the one before, so a stroke is also a chain of links. Every finger draws its own stroke.
   - At the page cap (1,000), a tap or a stroke paints the page under it, or the nearest page. The web also fills to the cap as it grows. Before this, a tap on empty space at the cap did nothing, which is what the user ran into when trying another object after hearts.
   - Measured with two crossing strokes: 60 Hz held, p95 17.3 ms, one 34 ms frame.

A line in the chosen colour says what the next tap brings in. A newcomer starts with log quality 1.5, so it grows out of a speck into sight.

The first version brought newcomers in at random, every 25 s or through a button. The user called that logic wrong, and it was removed.

**Strains.** A strain is a colour and an object brought in together. At first an object belonged to its colour, so a pink GPU brought in after pink hearts turned every pink heart into a GPU, and a newcomer in a colour already around could not spread into it. Now the same colour with two objects makes two strains, and each can take over the other's pages.

**Passing it on.** Every page that has fully taken on a strain exposes the pages it touches: pressed against it in the raft (맞닿음, the default), linked to it (링크), or both. Each exposed page catches the strain at `rate` per exposing neighbour per second.

**Taking it on.** It takes `takeOn` seconds to take the strain on, and only then passes it on. With 모양도 전파, the page also melts into the strain's object once half taken on. A stronger strain can take it over.

**Easing between strains.** The colour shown eases towards the strain's, with a time constant of a third of `takeOn`. The amount of colour eases towards 1 while a page has a strain and towards 0 once it has none. The object stays as it is until the new strain is half taken on.

This fixes three things the user saw with several strains about ("너무 갑작스럽게 애니메이션 없이"). A page taken from one strain by another used to:

- switch its colour at once;
- drop its amount to 0 and raise it again, so it flickered colour → silver → colour;
- morph back to its own object between the two strains' objects.

A taken page now passes through the colours between, for example pink → lavender → teal (checked in the browser and by a test).

**Losing it again.** With `keep` > 0, a page loses its strain after about that many seconds (SIS dynamics).

**Tuning.** At rate 0.6 and 2.5 s the whole raft turned in about 20 s. The defaults are now 0.1 and 3.5 s, so a strain moves a few pages at a time.

Only objects take a strain's colour. Under 전파 the pages are always drawn as objects, even when the object panel shows them as bubbles.

## Rocket → Starship (`objects/models.ts`)

User: the 🚀 did not read as a "hyper real spacex rocket". It is now the Starship ship:

- a 9 m × 50 m stainless cylinder with an ogive nose;
- two forward flaps and two broad aft flaps;
- the chine along its side;
- six Raptor bells: three sea-level, three vacuum;
- weld seams.

The weld seams are spaced every other 1.8 m ring. A seam is thinner than a field texel, so it is drawn a texel wide, and every ring together read as an accordion's ribs.

The other objects are unchanged. Adding and removing objects is expected next (user).
