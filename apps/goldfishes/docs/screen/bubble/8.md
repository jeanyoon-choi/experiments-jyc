## 액체 막 (`render/film.ts`)

The material is bubble/1's soap film, applied to everything drawn in the material — the raft's spheres (`render/foam.ts`) and the objects (`objects/object-pass.ts`) — through the shared shading (`render/shading.ts`):

- **Clear.** What lies behind (배경) shows through, dimmed by 12%, as bubble/1's.
- **Thickness.** Drains towards black film at the top and is folded by two layers of slow currents (bubble/1's recipe: 900 nm, folds, swirl).
- **Colour.** Thin-film interference at three wavelengths, mostly tempered to grey (간섭색 0.3). It pales further towards grazing angles, so a round object's wide rim glows rather than bands into a rainbow.
- **Light where seen edge-on.** Walls, free faces and an object's outline are bright lines.
- **Reflections.** The look's surroundings are reflected with a Fresnel weight. The key light stands in for bubble/1's window; a photographed room shows only if one is on.
- **Nearly flat.** As SCC bubble/1, a sphere's normal leans only by what one device pixel of its cap would. The drainage reads across a flat film, and only the last pixels at its walls turn. 구면 (bubble/1's 반사 곡면) rounds it towards a drop. Objects are flattened the same way, so their middles face the camera and only their rims turn away.

A first pass used a true spherical cap and the objects' full normals. It read as dark glossy rubber with wide rainbow rims and one large highlight per object, not as SCC bubble/1. It was corrected in the browser against SCC bubble/1 side by side.

**Dye.** 막 물들임 tints the film with the coat's hue at full brightness; a dark coat would only muddy it. 전파's strains and a logo's brand colour dye it in the same way. A black brand (Apple, X, TikTok, OpenAI) dyes nothing: a film cannot be black. 투명도 0 turns the film into an opaque liquid of its dye.

**Melting.** While an object melts, its film's colours run (in place of the coat's 수은화).

**액체** (0–1) blends the film with bubble/7's coat; 0 is bubble/7 exactly. The 재질 chips set 0 or 1. The film's sliders sit in 비주얼 → 액체 막: 액체, 막 두께, 간섭색, 소용돌이, 막 흐름 속도, 투명도, 막 물들임, 가장자리 빛, 돔 깊이, 구면.

## 오브제 그룹 (`objects/groups.ts`)

What a page can be shown as is a **group** of kinds:

- 테크 하이프 and SNS are the groups the earlier trials produced. They are test vocabularies, not the work's settled one.
- 테크 로고 is new.

`groups.ts` is the single registry. Each kind has its label, its feed keyword, how its field is made (`inflated` · `model` · `mark`) and whether it is on at the start. The following all read it:

- the 페이지 and 왔다갔다 하는 것 choices;
- each group's chips;
- the 랩 and 전파's object chips;
- the feed's vocabulary.

Adding a group is one entry, plus paths in `marks.ts` for marks. Before this, the labels were listed three times (objects, feed, panels), and the shown kinds were a hand-written list.

**테크 로고** is 14 symbol marks: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek, Apple, Meta, NVIDIA, X, Tesla, Windows, Instagram, TikTok, Bitcoin and Android.

- **The marks.** SCC bubble/3's 기술 surface had them as flat marks on balls of their brand colour; here each is the object itself. They come from simple-icons 16.25 (CC0 data; the marks remain their owners'). OpenAI and Windows use the paths SCC bubble/3 used.
- **Wordmarks are left out.** A mark must read as a shape, so Samsung, VMware and the like are not used.
- **How a mark becomes an object** (`objects/marks.ts`, `fields.ts` `fieldOfMark`). The SVG path is flattened, curves and arcs included, and filled with the non-zero rule as SVG fills it. It is then blown up as one balloon: ∇²u = −1, height 2√u, as SNS's heart is. Strokes swell into tubes and lobes into pillows.
- **Fit.** The furthest point of each mark reaches 1.18 of the disc.
- **Baking.** Each takes 120–250 ms in the field workers.
- **Brand colour.** 오브제 → 로고의 브랜드 색 (default 0.7) colours each logo, blended between the two logos a morph runs between.

The pages open as 테크 로고, the group under test; bubble/7 opened on 테크 하이프. The portions stay SNS.

## Simplified and made modular

- **`render/foam.ts`** keeps the raft's passes only.
  - The material shared with the objects (`COAT`, `SHADING`, the `Look` and its defaults, the key's direction) moved to `render/shading.ts`.
  - The film is `render/film.ts`.
  - `foam.ts` re-exports what its importers used.
- **`objects/objects.ts`** derives the pages' and portions' choices, the labels and the defaults from the group registry.
- **`objects/fields.ts`.** Meshes and marks share one surface → field step (`fieldOfSurface`) and one membrane solver (`solveMembrane`).
- **Field baking** puts first the kinds that are wanted on screen and not yet baked (`object-pass.ts` `shown`). With 14 more kinds, what is shown no longer waits behind the rest.
- **Film detail follows size.** A film under 28 CSS px in radius skips the second fold; under 9 px it skips the folds altogether, keeping the drainage and one swirl. Larger films follow bubble/1's recipe exactly.

## 금붕어 1,000 (2026-11-03)

The user asked for up to 1,000 fish with no cost to the frame. The 수 slider now goes to 1,000 (`MAX_FISH`); the opening school stays 380. The fish's look and behaviour are unchanged:

- **The step is cheaper and gives the same result.** A page whose whole hold circle lies under its neighbours is found once per step, from the arcs they cover (`buried()`), so it is neither scored nor searched. The fish skip pages they could never reach. A deterministic benchmark's state hash is the same as before (800 pages, 380 fish). CPU per step went from 10.9 to 2.7 ms at 380 fish, and is 6.7 ms at 1,000.
- **The step runs in a worker** (`fish/school-thread.ts`, `fish/school-worker.ts`). The frame loop uses `SchoolThread` as it used `GoldfishSchool`. Each frame's discs, ranks, links and bubble colours go to the worker, and the fish come back packed. Buffers are transferred, not copied. The fish drawn are one frame behind the bubbles. Without a worker (in node tests, or if the worker fails to load), the same step runs on the main thread. A test checks that this path matches the school stepped directly, fish for fish. The console logs once when the worker starts.
- **The scene draws more cheaply.** Matrices are written straight into the instance buffers, and only the fish there are uploaded. Body and fin colours are one buffer each, shared across their parts. Below body scale 0.78, a lighter geometry is drawn. Its outline lies within 0.13 px of the full one's at the opening size 0.65, and its vertices are shared.

Checking frame rate at 1,000 fish needs a clean browser: one visible bubble/8 tab, nothing else rendering. On 2026-11-03 other agents' tabs were running, so it was not measured.
