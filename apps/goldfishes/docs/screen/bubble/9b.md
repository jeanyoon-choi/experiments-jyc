- Code: none of its own — `components/screen/bubble/9/clock.tsx` renders bubble/9 opening on the 시계 morph style.

bubble/9 and bubble/9b are one codebase: every edit to bubble/9 (전파, materials, objects…) is in both. They differ only in which of `MORPH_STYLES` (`objects/morph/liquid.ts`) they open on, and either can switch to the other in 랩 → 액체 → 모핑 방식:

| | 금붕어 (bubble/9) | 시계 (bubble/9b) |
|---|---|---|
| A page moves on | once the fish have watched it 1 fish-second and its morph has set | on its own beat every 7 s |
| Morph | 8 s | 8 s |
| Between morphs | set solid | never quite set: the outline sways on a melt floor 0.45 |
| Setting | shape and melt stop together | the sway rings down (0.7 s, 1.6 Hz), the mirror leaves last |

Where they differ in code: `MORPH_STYLES` and `styleOf`; `frame-loop.ts turnPages` (how a page's turn advances); `morph.ts motion` (ring-down); the object shader's `residue` floor. Once the user picks one, delete the other style's row and those branches, `clock.tsx`, this file and the registry entry.
