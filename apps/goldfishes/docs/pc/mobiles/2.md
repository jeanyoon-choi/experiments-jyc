The second of NOTES 1023's three screens: one phone per goldfish. The user wanted a fast, never-still spectacle of a phone economy, with the goldfish trapped inside it. What mobiles/1 gave was "a collection of dull phone chunks". This trial makes the motion the unit of design, and the GPU the unit of drawing.

## Rendering (`gl/`, `paint/`, `phone/scene.ts`)

**The compositor.** One WebGL2 canvas draws the whole field in one instanced draw a frame, about 1,800 quads.

- **Quads.** Every bar, post, banner, card, heart and fish is a quad: a rect in its group's space. It is textured from the atlas or a solid colour, then rounded, clipped to a rect (a feed's viewport) and clipped to its phone's glass.
- **Groups carry the motion.** A group's motion is a pose eased into another between two moments: offset, scale x/y, rotation, opacity, about an origin. The vertex shader evaluates it from the time uniform through up to five parents (item → scroll → app → phone).
- **The CPU writes a group only when a motion begins.** An interrupted motion restarts from its exact current pose (`model/motion.ts` mirrors the shader's eases), so nothing jumps.
- **Eases:** out, inOut, an iOS-like spring, a flick's exponential coast, in, and a stiffer snap.
- **Strip tiles.** A tile can be a strip of frames, which the shader plays from the clock: the goldfish's tail beat. It costs no CPU.

**Tiles.**

- **Painting.** Each app's look is a set of pure Canvas2D painters (`apps/<id>/paint.ts`). Workers (`paint/worker.ts`, 2–4 of them) run them at the size the phones are shown (device px per pt, ≤ 2). A worker returns premultiplied pixels.
- **Reuse.** A spec painted once is shared by every phone. Content repeats on purpose (12 ambient and 6 strike variants per keyword, language and mode): the same ready-made on many phones (Warhol).
- **The atlas** (`gl/atlas.ts`): 12 layers of 2048², cut into columns as wide as a glass. A tile takes the first free interval; least-recently-used tiles that no glass shows are freed one by one.

**Measured on this Mac, 1470 × 759 at DPR 2, 64 phones, demo feed at 150 strikes/s, steady state over 8 s:**

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Frame rate | 60 fps |
| p95 frame | 17 ms |
| Worst frame | 18 ms |
| Quads | 1,758 |
| CPU a frame | uploads 1.5 ms, phones 0.6 ms, packing 0.8 ms, draw call 0.1 ms |

All 64 phones are lit within about 10 s of loading. The GPU budget (60 Hz, DPR up to 2) was approved by the user on 2026-10-28.

**Bugs found while measuring (fixed):**

| Bug | Fix |
|---|---|
| `UNPACK_PREMULTIPLY_ALPHA`, left on after an atlas upload, multiplied the groups' float texels by their 4th value (a parent index of −1). Nothing was drawn. | No premultiply flag at all now. |
| Uploading `ImageBitmap`s into a texture array took the browser's slow path: 40–60 ms a frame for 8 tiles. | Workers send raw pixels, uploaded within a 4 ms budget. |
| A GPU-backed `OffscreenCanvas` cost a readback of about 10 ms a tile. | `willReadFrequently`; a tile now takes 3–9 ms. |
| Whole-layer atlas eviction jammed: every layer held a tile on some glass. | Columns with tiles freed one by one. |
| A chat view refilled itself without end, about 100 messages a second. | Chats grow only when a message comes. |

## A phone (`phone/phone.ts`, `views.ts`)

**Owner (`model/persona.ts`).** Each phone has an owner, fixed by its seat:

- dark or light (about half each);
- iPhone or Galaxy (about a quarter): punch hole, Material banner, Android lock clock;
- language: en 55%, ko 20%, ja 10%, es 15%;
- pace 0.75–1.35×;
- 6–9 apps, most used first;
- wallpaper hue;
- which of bubble/7's fish palettes its goldfish wears.

**Apps (`apps/`).** Thirteen hyper-real clones plus the system:

| App | Grammar |
|---|---|
| Instagram | feed |
| Reels, TikTok | full-screen clips swiped up |
| X | feed |
| Facebook | feed |
| LinkedIn | feed |
| Tinder | cards |
| Yelp | feed |
| Netflix | hero and rows |
| News | Apple News |
| Stocks | Robinhood-like |
| Deals | Temu-like |
| Messages | group chat |

- The system draws the status bar, banners, lock and home screens, a full-screen ad, the pay sheet, Screen Time's limit and the Dynamic Island's live activity.
- About a fifth of feed items are ads, Sponsored or Promoted (NOTES 1023: the attention economy's ad space).
- Facebook, LinkedIn, Yelp and News were written by one delegated agent; Netflix, Stocks, Deals and Messages by a second; TikTok, Reels and Tinder by a third, all against the Instagram/X painters as the contract.

**Never still.** Every phone is always in use. One goldfish, the thumb, does all of it:

- flicks the feed (an exponential coast), with the next flick starting before the coast ends;
- likes, with a burst of hearts;
- swipes clips up and double-taps them;
- throws Tinder cards aside;
- tapbacks a chat.

About every `hop` seconds it changes app, in one of four ways:

- taps a banner, and the app opens out of the banner's rect;
- swipes the home bar;
- uses the app switcher: cards, a slide, a pick;
- goes home: the app shrinks into its icon, the next one opens out of its own.

Ads slide up (Skip, or Shop now, then a pay sheet, then Face ID) at `ads` a minute.

**A strike is an interrupt, not a data insertion.**

- **Felt at once, in the same frame:** the whole device buzzes (a notification's haptic).
- **At most one painted answer per phone every 1.1 s ÷ pace:**
  - the post goes into this very app, just below the view, and the thumb flicks to it and likes it;
  - or a banner drops, iOS-stacked, and is opened at once (`open`) or pushed away;
  - a strong strike (rank > 0.72) also grows the Dynamic Island into a live activity.
- When painting falls behind (backlog > 90 tiles), strikes are felt, not painted.

## Field (`model/field.ts`, `phone/engine.ts`)

- **Ripple.** A strike of rank ≥ 0.55 spreads ring by ring to the phones around it (`wave`), 0.11 s a ring, as bubble/7's strains spread through the raft. The neighbours get a banner of the same keyword.
- **Surge (동시 알림).** When the field's strike rate jumps 1.7× above its 25 s average, every phone gets the same notification at the same instant. Cooldown 22 s.
- **Death** (NOTES 1020, 1023): too many strikes in 10 s (`overdose`, default 32; with the demo at 150 strikes/s the median phone takes 20, the busiest about 36, and at 28 eleven of 64 were dark at once, too many), or no new keyword for `stillness` s (default 14). Nothing dies while no screen feeds the field. Options (user: not kitsch, not a cliché, adjustable):
  - **꺼짐** (default):
    1. the goldfish turns into bubble/7's spent fish (grey, rolled onto its side, tail still) and sinks off the glass;
    2. the screen dims as an untouched phone's does, then goes dark;
    3. after `rest` s (default 2.5), the next strike wakes it: the lock screen lights, the notification lands, a new goldfish swims in and swipes it open.
  - **한도 무시**: Screen Time's limit sheet rises; the goldfish taps "Ignore Limit" and goes on.
  - **끔**: no death.
