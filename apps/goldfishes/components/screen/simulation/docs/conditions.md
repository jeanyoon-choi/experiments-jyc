## 1. The artist's conditions

### What the work is (notes)

- Goldfish are people: myopic, chasing the latest trend, hype, FOMO, dopamine. Bubbles are platforms and their algorithms; a bubble carries a sign. Both optimise only what is in front of them, with no long aim; from their feedback comes what nobody controls (1106, 1115).
- "움직이는 버블들은 일종의 도파민이다." bubble/9's experiments were PageRank; the work's own logic is agent-based modelling, "한 키워드에서 다음 키워드로". "핵심 법칙: Occam's Razor, Simple Rule" (1111).
- A bubble is an empty place; the sign on it keeps changing place. What it is does not matter; that it changes does (1017, 1020).
- Goldfish have a lifespan: past a certain dopamine they die and a new shoal comes from every side, like mayflies (Hirst's *A Thousand Years*) (1020).
- New things come only from the audience, who sit where a platform's owner sits and do not control the whole (system map v2.1).
- Later: goldfish and bubbles that differ (a disposition each), then a slow semantic layer (1115). Not now.
- Transcribed handwriting, unconfirmed: about 500 bubbles should be makeable (1118); a low line that erupts at a threshold, a phase transition (1123); a finger's curve laid as bubbles with goldfish round them, "finger skating" (1123); the extremes — every bubble bursting, or bubbles filling the screen until the fish suffocate (1115); the states to be told apart: steady / 뒤죽박죽 / 대세가 바뀌는 중 / 지각 변동 (1115).

### The 25 corrections (model document, part 3; the artist's, 2026-11-14/16)

1. Occam's razor. Do not fix the answer and fit to it; the wanted scene is used only to judge.
2. A simple rule; make, look, fix, repeat. Think what a formula will produce before coding it.
3. One optimisation function for each agent. No temperament system bolted on. The more stimuli, the shorter the attention. One stroke is one keyword.
4. **A sharp change must be seen within 10–30 s. A keyword the audience draws works at once and spreads.** A large bubble must not split by itself to hand a trend on.
5. No devices piled on.
6. **A new keyword does not take the board from the start. It grows gradually; some live, some die.**
7. **Nothing divides by itself without input. The more bubbles, the faster trends change.**
8. Do not reread the artist's words to suit; do not read an example as a specification.
9. **No rule that was not asked for (sight at a distance, creators, chasing, a pulling force). "바로 앞만 본다는 것은 정말로 바로 앞만 본다는 뜻이다."**
10. A cluster of bubbles, or any scene, comes only as a result.
11. Every correction and each version's point is added to the document each time.
12. No next version without a principle.
13. A bubble that reads the goldfish's minds is not myopic. A bubble sees its own count of fish and its neighbours; its next sign comes from a neighbour.
14. Korean in plain words, with a subject, with an example. For every agent.
15. **M6–M8 are dull because the liveliness of M1 and M3 is missing: bubbles changing position and place** (measured: it came from differences of size, bursting and pushing, not from their rules). Judge from captures of the real screen.
16. Uproar is fun, but only after input and a varied course. Uproar that goes on is static. There must be different states (uproar, stable, dominance) told apart by numbers. Not formulas by feel.
17. **With no input that makes a bubble the screen stays as it is; when a new bubble comes the flow changes with it.** Nothing tries a new keyword or changes state by itself.
18. A complex system has no fixed cause and effect: the same stroke may start a chain or nothing, by the number of agents, the ecology, the flow of the moment.
19. (M8's third) It does not come back from uproar to stable. New keywords too often fail to spread and die. What the artist sees on the screen must be pointed to by a number.
20. **(M8.1) A new trend spreads too fast and bubbles grow too fast; not dynamic, no uproar, dull; after many strokes it does not come back. Measure in 3–5 s units. Bubble size is absolute, not a share of a fixed whole. The number of goldfish is not fixed: few at first, more as keywords come in.**
21. A slow change of leader is a transition, not uproar: judge by direction, not amount.
22. Bursting is forced. (M8.3, without it, was then judged a failure; M9 was built on M8.2, which bursts.)
23. M8.3 is a failure; M9 on M8.2.
24. **A bubble's area, not its width, is in proportion to its fish.** The centre turning fast and small while the rim is large and steady is not wanted.
25. M9.1's first was too unlike M9: no uproar, no sharp movement, dull.

Found that weekend and still standing: where the screen rests is decided by how new keywords enter; bursting moves bubbles but hardly changes the flow; copying alone ends in one keyword (the voter model); size that follows the fish at once makes a trend look too fast (M8.2 slowed the heat's cooling from 1 to 0.3 a second for it); with a fixed number of fish many strokes never settle (sand that cannot leave the pile) — hence M9's fish that come and go.

### The body, from bubble/1–9 (the artist's verdicts)

- Rich and poor: solved in the rule, never by a cap on size or a patch that shrinks bubbles. Too equal is "pure noise 처럼 정보량 없는"; "조금 더 빈부격차가 커야함". Accepted (bubble/6): the largest 17–22 times the median's width, a few large, a few dozen middle, many small, and it keeps changing. A model that barely moved: "거의 실패".
- Every bubble is still a bubble shown as an object; "지속해서 바뀌고, 그 어디에도 멈춰있지 않은". A page turns to its next object once the goldfish have watched it (no clock): the watched flow, the unwatched are read.
- Portions (흐름 버블) show the flow and morph from the source's shape to the target's.
- 전파: "finger skating 마냥 … 쭉 누르면서 … 여러 오브제 쫘르르", a newcomer every 30 px; its colour "차근차근히 전파". Newcomers at random, with no hand, were called wrong.
- The goldfish as bubble/9's, never rebuilt. A fish touching a page is attention landing on content.
- Reference feel: bubble/9's opening (preset A1): 800 pages, 380 fish, 속도 3, 새 페이지 0.85, 터지는 크기 80, 품질에 주는 영향 20 / 상한 9.1, 모핑 8 s.

### For `simulation/1` (the artist, 2026-11-24/25, in order)

1. The aim: bubble/9's feeling — a trend spreading, circulating, evolving — and its picture, from an agent-based model in place of PageRank, "dummy algorithm". M9 is a reference with its own problems, never a thing to install. Everything of bubble/9 that is not the logic stays as it is (materials, objects, morph, the fish's body and lifecycle, sound, panels, presets A1–B4); the flow bubbles are the visual core.
2. Nothing invented that is not on the screen or in the notes ("몸 없는 금붕어 … 왜 자꾸 없는 assumption을 만들어내?"). The model in plain words before it is built.
3. New bubbles only when the audience draws. A goldfish looks at bubbles near it. A bubble that changes takes the object of the neighbour that sent it the portion.
4. The stroke must be laid where it is drawn, be seen, and act ("인터랙션 자체가 전혀 안됨" when it came in at nothing).
5. The system's options in a panel of their own; versions one can go between, in modular code that does not grow complicated.
6. It must be playable on a Windows computer. Opening count: 200, then 1 (2026-11-25).
7. **A newcomer must not come in large; the whole school must not gather on one bubble.** With few bubbles the goldfish swim free in the tank and only some near a bubble are held; the number held shows it; with more bubbles more fish are held. A scaffold, not a patch.
8. **A bubble's size is in proportion to its goldfish.** Small at first; as bubbles multiply the cluster grows and so does the gap between rich and poor.
9. Common to every agent simulation: Occam's razor, the simplest rule, no rule grown complicated, no patch rules (땜빵) ([direction](../../../../docs/direction.md#principles-the-artists-from-those-corrections)).
10. **A goldfish swimming free is not worn down** (its strength is spent on watching). No mass death. The whole of death and life reviewed.
11. **Free swimming is slow; once a bubble has given it dopamine, the pace it has now** — so the two are told apart.
12. **A bubble growing by itself and goldfish going to it by themselves with no input is a contradiction.** One stroke must not take the whole screen, least of all at once ("아직 시작도 안했는데"). But bubbles too small for anything to be seen are not an answer either ("쪼끄맣고 재미도 없는"). Sizes must not jump ("크기도 glitchy").
13. Checking: long enough on the running screen, never by the number of passing tests; beside bubble/9.
14. Whether the screen rests with no input (bubble/9 never does): **yes, it rests** (2026-11-25, on the plan of 에이전트 3: "ㅇㅋ 이대로"). bubble/9's full picture is then the state while the audience keeps drawing.
15. **The core must be judged by a measure, and there was none** (2026-11-25, on 에이전트 3's first run): "전혀 골드피시의 핵심, bubble/9 비주얼의 핵심인 유행의 전파, 정신없는 오브제들 기호들의 파티, 대난리, 이런 느낌이 전혀 없는데, 너 이거에 대한 평가기준이 전혀 없음." A stroke's bubbles shrinking and dying is not the picture ("얘네들이 자연스럽게 작아지고 죽잖아").
16. **The going back and forth must stop** (same day): "왜 자꾸 왔다갔다하면서 문제가 해결 안되는건데? 너가 처음에 나보고 simulation 수식 이해 없어도 된다고 했지?" The lead's advice on 2026-11-24 that the formulas need not be understood was wrong.

## 2. What was found (analyses)

- **What bubble/9's feeling is made of** (code read, 2026-11-24): one conserved weight moved as portions; appeal that grows with rank (the rich get richer); quality drifting at random (the leaders keep changing with no input); tiring above 80 shares (nothing keeps the screen); new pages 0.85 a second; watching raises appeal and turns the object; links that reach anywhere. Table in [1](./1.md#what-bubble9s-feeling-is-made-of-the-starting-point-for-a-next-attempt).
- **One change at a time** (headless, 800 bubbles, [1](./1.md#the-agent-model-one-change-at-a-time)): of the eight places made local only two change the picture. Spreading nothing over all (row 4) dries 143–749 of 800 bubbles to specks; quality that does not wander (row 5) halves how fast the ten largest change (1.7 → 0.7 a second). Giving row 4's share to the touching bubbles brings the specks back to 10.
- **bubble/9 never rests because of exactly those two** (the dice and the spread), and neither is anybody's act. Under condition 17/14 the screen rests without input.
- **Cost** (M4, `?measure`): the rules cost almost nothing (web 0.6 ms, emit 0.9 ms); the frame is the renderer's, by the pixel (GPU 11 ms with 33 bubbles, 27–31 ms with a full raft; a quarter of the pixels halves it). A shoal of 380 free fish drawn together cost the school's worker 10.8 ms (bubble/9: 7.5 ms). Nothing was measured on the Windows computer.
- **Deaths**: bubble/9 kills about 290 goldfish a minute in the reference window (spent 107, crushed 162, shut in 98 in 60 s).
- **Size from the head count** (에이전트 2): with size following the fish in 1.5 s and fish seeing a new bubble from afar, one stroke drew 208 of 380 fish and the raft went to 55% of the screen in 10 s; with one fish's worth a bubble and 30 s to follow, a stroke stayed at 5% and nothing was seen to happen. The same failure as M8.1's (correction 20), then its opposite (correction 4).

## 4. 에이전트 3: the plan agreed on 2026-11-25

One currency, attention.

- **Goldfish** (one aim: to watch what bores it less). With no bubble in touch it drifts slowly and is not worn. It is held only by a bubble it *touches*; it sees none from afar. Held, it is bubble/9's goldfish (the pace, the watching, the wear, death and the new shoal), and can go only to bubbles at hand. It tires of the *objects* it has watched; when nothing at hand is better than swimming on, it lets go (as M9's fish had "off the screen" among its choices).
- **Bubble** (one aim: to hold more fish). Its area is the attention it has been given and not yet lost: it gains from each fish watching it and loses 15% a beat (bubble/9's number), so it follows over about 7 s; absolute, not a share. Portions between touching bubbles, a watched bubble taking its neighbour's object, tiring when too large: bubble/9's.
- **Audience**. A stroke is one new object, laid where it is drawn, at a starting size that is one setting (as every M model had).

Expected, to be checked, not promised: no input — still; one stroke — the fish there are held at once, the bubbles grow and flow within 10–30 s, then the fish tire of the one object and it thins, some bubbles living and some not; many strokes of many objects — long holds, large objects, deaths; drawing kept up — bubble/9's picture; input stopped — back to quiet.

Risks named beforehand: the first attempt also had size from the fish and froze with only the rim alive (now: portions carry size inward, fish die and are replaced, tiring stays — whether that is enough is the largest unknown); whether the fish alone open the gap to 17–22 times; four settings remain (a fish's worth, the letting-go threshold, how long an object is remembered, a stroke's size).

How it is checked (the model document's order): five minutes without input; the same stroke in different situations, the spread of the answers in 3–5 s numbers and captures; many strokes then none; drawing kept up, beside bubble/9 in the same window. Shown to the artist only when numbers and captures both hold; a step that fails is reported, not tuned past.

## 5. Why the work kept going back and forth, and what changes (2026-11-25)

What happened on 11-24/25, six times over: a rule put in plain words, the lead's arithmetic of what it would do, a build in the full body, a minute's look, a surprise, a value pushed the other way. Too fast, then dead; the whole screen, then specks.

Why:
1. **No measure of the thing aimed at.** The tracker counts sizes, deaths and frames. Nothing says whether a trend is spreading, whether the screen is a crowded party of signs, whether there is uproar; and the agent versions were never put through the same numbers as bubble/9 in the same run. So "done" was whatever did not look broken for a minute.
2. **The behaviour wanted lives in a narrow band** (the notes' own point: a threshold, a phase transition). Each value is not "more or less" of an effect; across the band the screen flips from frozen to runaway. Stepping by guess crosses it every time.
3. **Two directions at once.** Down from bubble/9 one change at a time (the ladder, the only time a result was explained) and up from an empty tank (에이전트 2, 3). The lead switched between them as the opening count changed.
4. **The formulas were never written down or shared.** The lead told the artist they need not be understood, then planned in words whose consequences nobody could check (correction 2: think what a formula will produce before coding it).
5. The model document's part 1 §2 names the same failure for the weekend of 11-14: "결과를 재지 않고 눈으로만 봤다 … 규칙을 넣었다 뺐다만 반복".

What changes (proposed to the artist; see [1](./1.md) for whether it was taken up):
- **A scorecard first**, each line a number measured on bubble/9 itself: the party (how much of the screen, how many objects above a size, how many kinds, the largest against the median), the restlessness (objects changing a second, the ten largest replaced, neighbours changing, portions under way), the spreading (a new object's share of bubbles and of area over the 30 s after it comes; how often the leading object changes), the uproar (the document's 4 s judgement by direction). And the conditions as pass or fail (still without input; one stroke under a share of the screen in its first 10 s; no size jumping; back from uproar).
- **One harness** that runs any version without a screen through fixed scenes (no input, one stroke, ten strokes, drawing kept up) and prints the scorecard, so a value is swept in minutes and the band is found before the body is touched.
- **One direction**: from the picture down. bubble/9's numbers, then one change at a time, each kept only if the scorecard holds.
- **The model as a few equations with what they predict**, in this folder, in plain words beside them.
- The screen is shown only when the scorecard passes, beside bubble/9.
- It may be that the conditions and bubble/9's numbers cannot all hold together. Then what is reported is which condition costs which number, for the artist to choose.
