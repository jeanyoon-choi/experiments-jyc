The migration. The weekend's model lived in one HTML file per version, each with its rules, its drawing and its panel in one script. Here its rules are a module that runs with or without a screen, so the next version can put them under `bubble`'s body and so a change can be measured before it is looked at.

```text
question   do M9's rules, moved out of the gallery page, still behave as the model document measured?
baseline   M9 as in docs/model/goldfishes-all-versions.html#m9 (its script is M9.1's with three switches off)
mutation   none to the rules. Drawing is apart from them (view.ts), the controls a table (controls.ts). Added: a seeded random stream.
invariants every rule, default and constant of M9; the order of a step (fish, audience and heat, copying, bursting, sizes)
evidence   the three situations the document measured, against its figures (below)
```

## What is here

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |

| `view.ts`, `index.tsx` | The route: the model drawn as the gallery drew it (a keyword a colour, a sign a shape, a goldfish a dot), a finger drawn across the field a stroke. It opens on M9. |
| `controls.ts`, `panels/`, `ui/` | The controls, laid out as `bubble/9`'s (its pieces copied): the versions at the bottom left (‹ ›, `←` `→` or `[` `]` step through M8, M8.2, M9, M9.1, each starting a new screen; "직접 조정" once a value is moved), three option panels at the bottom right (금붕어, 버블, 관객 · 시작). Every parameter is there, with the gallery's own labels and ranges, as one table in `controls.ts`; a value acts on the running screen, except the four a screen starts from. `X` hides them. |
| `model.test.ts` | What must hold: no keyword without a stroke, a fish moves only within its bubble's view, a stroke's bubbles and fish, the same seed the same world. |

One engine (user, 2026-11-18: Occam's razor). M3, the version whose feel the artist liked most, is not carried over as a second engine: its rules cannot be reached by these parameters (one freshness shared by all fish, new signs without input), and it stays runnable in the gallery file.

## Measured (2026-11-18)

Three seeds each, steps of 0.05 s. A stroke is a straight line of nine bubbles at a seeded place.

| | no input, 3 min: still | one stroke: half the fish in | largest bubble | still again after | ten strokes in 2 min: uproar | fish at most |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M9 | 91–96% (fish 150 → 23–26) | 5–13 s | 2.5–2.9× | 3–62 s | 72–76% | 463–478 |
| M9.1 | 94–96% (→ 23–36) | 3–12 s | 2.7–2.9× | 30–38 s | 68–81% | 485–503 |
| M8.2 | 77–100% | 10–14 s | 2.6–2.8× | 33–131 s | 53–72% | 380 (fixed) |
| M8 | 0–25% | 10–11 s, once never | 9–11× | never | 92–95% | 380 |

Against the document's own figures: M8.2 without input 80% still, half the fish in 13–17 s, largest 2.8×, 9–11 bursts in 4 s (here 9–10), still again after 64–117 s; M8 in uproar without input; M9 without input 98% still with the fish falling to about 21, uproar 75–84% under ten strokes. The port agrees with these within the spread between seeds. Two figures are not comparable as they stand: "largest" for M8 (the document gives heat over a reference heat, this gives area, and M8 shares one fixed area), and the fish at most (the document's 410–430 for M9 depends on its strokes' length, which it does not give).

**After ten strokes, how long until the screen is still again** depends on how long the strokes were (two seeds each, ten minutes of nothing after):

| | strokes of 3 bubbles | strokes of 9 bubbles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M9 | 2.5–3.1 min; fish at most 336–407 | 5.5–7.3 min; fish at most 472–473 |
| M9.1 | 2.0–3.3 min | 4.9–6.4 min |

The document's "5–7 minutes" for M9 and "2–3 minutes" for M9.1 both fall inside this. A stroke leaves its bubbles on the screen for good, and each is its own shape of the keyword, so a longer stroke leaves more that is not yet familiar. The length of a stroke was not a parameter of the model; it acts like one.

M8.1 was a preset here until 2026-11-19 (user: taken out). It was M8.2's rules with M8's sizes (a fixed total area, contrast 2, capacity 10) and a bubble counting the fish still swimming to it; measured before removal: 97% still without input, largest bubble 25–27×, uproar 71–77% under ten strokes. Its one switch (`countSwimming`) went with it. It stays runnable in the gallery file.

## What this does not show

- Nothing new about how it looks: the route draws what the gallery drew. Checked in the browser (2026-11-18): it runs, rests without input, and a stroke's keyword spreads over the field within about half a minute. The gallery's screens were judged dull by the artist beside `bubble/9`, and these are the same rules. Seen side by side on 2026-11-18 (lead, in the browser): M8.2 onward hold 60–75 bubbles of nearly one size, because area follows the fish a bubble holds and it bursts at four times the mean, so no bubble is ever more than twice another's width; `bubble/9` shows a few very large objects among hundreds of small ones and recomposes itself every 5–10 s with no input. That is an observation, not yet a cause of the dullness.
- The thresholds of the four states are still the document's first guesses.
- Not compared step for step with the gallery script: the random streams differ, so only the measured situations were compared.
