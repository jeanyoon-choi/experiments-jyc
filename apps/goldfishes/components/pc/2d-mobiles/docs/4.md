## 치환 (`filters/sign.ts`, `gl/shaders.ts`, `phone/scene.ts`)

The reference is SCC `/mobiles/2` with substitution/3 ([notes](../../../../scc/docs/experiments/mobile/substitution.md)): every part of a clone becomes a white cell with a black frame and one capital word stretched to fill it. Seen in the browser there before building (2026-11-25, 3 × 11, 규율).

**What differs, and why.** SCC reads a DOM, so its cells are a name, a handle, a picture, an icon. Here a glass has no DOM: it is a stack of painted tiles moved by the compositor (item → scroll → app → mobile). A cell here is one **layer** of that stack — an app's bar, a post, a clip, a card, a message, a banner, an ad, the pay sheet, the home and lock screens — so it is coarser than SCC's, and it is exactly what moves. The cell is drawn by the fragment shader in the quad's own place, so it scrolls, is clipped, thrown and faded as the layer is, with no repaint and no lag. The goldfish, hearts, the tap ring and the status bar are no layer and stay as they are.

**The word** is a tile of its own (`filters/word-paint.ts`: white capitals scaled until their ink fills the tile), stretched again over the cell. Three readings:

| 칸이 읽는 것 | A layer with a keyword | An app's bar | Other layers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 키워드 (default) | the keyword as the main screen names it (AI, GPU, TESLA) | the app's name, as under 레이어 | their 레이어 word |
| 레이어 | POST (feed), CLIP (pager), CARD (stack), MESSAGE (chat); AD if sponsored | the app's name | NOTIFICATION, AD, PAY, HOME, LOCK, LIMIT |
| 명령어 | by app, from SCC's 규율 words: Instagram PERFORM, TikTok REPEAT, X REPORT as there; the other apps read the same way here (an authored reading, as SCC's is) | RETURN | COMPLY, OBEY, PLAN, SCHEDULE |

- 키워드 is what circulates: the keyword of the bubble a goldfish touched, wherever it landed on that glass and on its neighbours (the ripple and surge of /2). The artist's notes sketch screens filled with one word (1118: "AI AI AI AI", "Blockchain Blockchain"; "뉴스: 새 트렌드 → 전부 그거").
- 레이어 is the wrapper: the same event in every app, each in its own wrapper (NOTES 1017, "껍데기만 다르고, 구조는 동일하다"; 1030, how it circulates rather than what).
- 키워드 색 gives each keyword of the screen's vocabulary its own hue, spread evenly round the wheel (`phone/engine.ts` `hueOfKeyword`), so one keyword's spread can be followed across the field under any of the three readings.
- A tile's keyword rides on its spec (`TileSpec.keyword`, no part of the tile's identity), set where the mobile makes a post, a banner, an ad, a pay sheet or a live activity.
- 자율 전환 (SCC's voter model on the grid) was built first and removed at the user's word the same day; UI만 took its place.
- **A switch spreads as a rumour** (user, 2026-11-25: not one fade for all, but shorter fades, each mobile on its own, propagating as a rumour does, within 3 s; the same day, twice as fast: 1.5 s and a 0.175 s fade, which is what runs now, not measured again). `filters/rumour.ts`: about one mobile in forty hears first; each that has heard tells the mobiles beside, above and below it, each telling taking its own time (0.4–1.6, drawn once); a mobile hears by the quickest chain, and the times are scaled so the last hears at `SUB_WITHIN − SUB_FADE` (2.65 s at first, 1.325 s now). On hearing, a mobile fades in `SUB_FADE` s (`SUB_FADE`), its content and the interface around it apart, so between any two of 원본, 치환, 콘텐츠만 and UI만 what comes fades in while what goes fades out. What the route opens with is there at once. A change of 칸이 읽는 것 is not faded. The first version, one 0.6 s fade for the whole field, was replaced by this.
- Measured (100 mobiles, the engine's own per-mobile values every 0.3 s): 원본 → 치환 substituted 0, 4, 26, 47, 59, 72, 82, 87, 95, 99, 100 mobiles, with 2–23 in mid-fade at a time; 치환 → UI만 and UI만 → 원본 ran the same way, done by 3.0 s. A capture of the field mid-switch was not taken.

## 물결 (`gl/post.ts`, `phone/engine.ts` `after`)

In place of the goldfish shown outright, the water it moves. The goldfish is its only source: a touch on the glass (the same moment as /2's tap ring) is a drop, and swimming leaves a wake. What is seen is the field through that surface: bent by its slope (never past its own glass), brighter where a crest gathers the light and a little dimmer in a trough. With 금붕어 숨김, only this is left of the fish.

Three ways of making the surface (물결 방식), after the user's correction (2026-11-25: fine at the centre, unnatural towards the edge, "just a paper texture"; reflected waves are not needed):

| 방식 | How | Seen (1389 × 868, DPR 2, demo feed, `ripple=0.6`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 고리 (default) | No surface is stepped. Each drop is worked out where it is shown (`ringsAt`): a short train of waves on a ring growing at 230 pt/s, lower as it spreads and gone after 1.9 s. A touch is a ring of 1; swimming leaves one of 0.3 every 240 pt. The last 8 drops of each mobile are kept. In pt and seconds, so the same at any size and frame rate. Nothing comes back from an edge. | Clean round rings from each touch, fading before or at the edge; no grain. 24 and 100 mobiles. |
| 열린 물 | The surface is a height field at half the canvas's resolution, stepped once a frame by the wave equation (`STEP`), nine neighbours. Towards a glass's edge the water is thick, so a wave dies there. | Rings and wakes with more of water's irregularity, fading towards the edge; no grain. 24 mobiles. |
| 어항 | The same surface with edges that turn a wave back: the first version. | The first version is what the user rejected. It now has nine neighbours and the new light, and was not looked at again. |

- **Why the first read as paper.** Each glass is small, so reflected waves crossed each other many times before fading, most of all near the edges; four neighbours made rings square and grainy; and the surface was lit from one side, as an embossing is.
- **Light.** By how sharply the surface bends, not by which way it faces, and a trough dims at most 0.1.
- The two stepped ways run per frame, so they are faster on a 120 Hz display, and need a half-float render target (`EXT_color_buffer_float` or `…_half_float`); 고리 needs neither.

**Tuned by looking (2026-11-25).**

| What | Seen |
| --- | --- |
| 어항: touch 1, wake up to 0.5 a frame, fading 0.988 | Failed: the water never rested; every glass boiled like crushed foil. |
| 어항: 0.7, 0.04, 0.978 | Failed the other way: a faint wobble, rings only just visible. |
| 어항: 1.2, 0.1, 0.985 | Rings read at the centre; paper towards the edge (the user's correction). |
| 고리: slope × 5.5, bend × 34, a wake ring every 110 pt | Failed: black and white bands over the interface, chains of circles. |
| 고리: × 3, × 9, every 240 pt, a trough dims 0.1 at most | Clean but faint. |
| 고리: × 4, × 15 (kept) | Rings read over the interface without covering it. |
| 열린 물: bend × 1 | Too faint to see. × 5 (kept): rings and wakes read. |

## 어안 (`gl/post.ts` `SHOW`)

Each glass through a lens, in the same pass as the water.

- **유리 가운데**: a barrel. The middle of the glass is large, the middle of each edge stays where it was, the edges bow outward and the corners run off the glass, so a glass reads as a bowl.
- **금붕어**: a bulge as wide as the glass that travels with the goldfish, largest where the goldfish is. The goldfish's place is the thumb's own (`Phone.fishAt`: the thumb's group in the device's, as the compositor places it), sent as a small texture.
- A glass shows itself only: where the lens looks past its edge there is nothing.

## Open

- A cell is a whole layer. A post's picture, name and caption as cells of their own (SCC's grain) would need the painters to paint cells, and a repaint on every switch.
- 키워드 left an app's bars as empty cells; they read as margins above and below (artist, 2026-11-25), so a layer with no keyword now reads its 레이어 word. Whether the bars should read something of the keyword instead is open.
- The water stays on its own glass. Whether a wave should cross to a neighbour (a keyword's ripple does) was not tried.
- 열린 물 and 어항 were not timed apart after the change to nine neighbours.
