## What differs from /2

- **No device.** No bezel ring, no rim, in every mode (`phone/phone.ts`).
- **No header.** The status bar, the Dynamic Island and the Android punch hole sit in one group (`header`) that is hidden by default. The island's live activity does not run while it is hidden.
- **The glass is cut.** A band can be cut off the top and the bottom of every glass (`u_crop` in `gl/shaders.ts`). By default the top 54 pt is cut, where the status bar was, so no empty band is left.
- **Square corners** by default.
- **The grid covers the screen** (user, 2026-11-24: /2's philosophy is fitting, with room left above and below; /3's is to fill, nothing left on any side, running over the screen). `model/layout.ts` `fitGrid`: whole mobiles, the grid as wide and as tall as the viewport at least, centred; what runs over is cut by the edge. Columns are those that run over least, full rows first. /2's fitting stays as an option.
- **The arrangement is an option**, from the values below.

## 배치 (fourth trigger, bottom right)

Defaults are in `model/tuning.ts` `TUNING_DEFAULTS`; the sliders' ranges in `LAYOUT_RANGES`. A changed value is written to the address, so an arrangement survives a reload and can be shared. All of them apply at once, with no rebuild unless the mobiles' size changes.

| Option | Query | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 화면 채우기 | `cover` | 1 | 1 (채움): the grid covers the viewport and runs over its edges. 0 (맞춤): the whole grid fits inside, as /2. |
| 열 수 | `columns` | 0 | Columns of the grid. 0: chosen by the rule above. |
| 가로 간격 | `gapX` | 2 | Between mobiles sideways, % of a mobile's width. |
| 세로 간격 | `gapY` | 2 | Between mobiles downwards, % of a mobile's width. |
| 좌우 여백 | `marginX` | 0 | Left and right of the grid, % of the viewport's width; kept clear when the grid runs over. |
| 상하 여백 | `marginY` | 0 | Above and below the grid, % of the viewport's height. |
| 모서리 둥글기 | `radius` | 0 | A glass's corner radius, pt (a mobile's own is 55). |
| 위 잘라내기 | `cropTop` | 54 | pt cut off the top of every glass. |
| 아래 잘라내기 | `cropBottom` | 0 | pt cut off the bottom (the home bar's band is 34). |
| 상태 표시줄·노치 | `header` | 0 | 1 shows the status bar and the island or punch hole (set `cropTop=0` with it). |

The number of mobiles is 폰 수 (`phones`) in the 필드 menu, 1–100 here (user, 2026-11-24; /2 stops at 64). The default is 100 (user, 2026-11-25).

- The screen sends 64 slots (socket feed/1, unchanged). Past 64 mobiles, seat n shares the slot of seat n − 64 and takes the same strike 0.2–0.8 s later, so the two are never in step (`phone/engine.ts`).
- `MAX_PHONES` in `gl/shaders.ts` is 100; 한 대 and 손 step through 100 seats.

## Picture, and no black screen (user, 2026-11-25)

- **Resolution.** The canvas is drawn at the display's own pixel ratio, up to 3 (`dpr`; /2 stops at 2, which a 2.2 display stretches).
- **선명도 (`sharp`, 필드 menu, default 1.25).** Tiles are painted finer than they are shown, so text stays sharp while a feed scrolls between pixels.
- **The atlas has room.** 20 layers of 2048² (12 in /2; about 135 MB more GPU memory). The tile size is also held under what the atlas can hold for the mobiles shown (`GLASSES_A_MOBILE` = 6 whole glasses each, `phone/engine.ts`): with `sharp=1.5` on 12 layers the atlas filled and mobiles waited black.
- **No mobile goes dark.** 죽음 is 끔 by default (`death=off`); 꺼짐 and 한도 무시 stay in the 금붕어 menu.
- A mobile is still black for the first seconds after loading, until its first screen is painted.
- 금붕어 크기 goes to 3×, default 2×.
- **X** hides the menus and the mode switch, and brings them back: screen/bubble/9's setup, copied (`controls/hidden-ui.ts`). It reads the key's position (KeyX), so it works under a Korean layout; the first version read the letter and did not.

## Checks

- Typecheck and ESLint clean; `node --test components/pc/2d-mobiles/3/model/model.test.ts`: /2's 9 tests pass.
- Seen in the browser (lead, 2026-11-24, 1110 × 690, `?source=demo`): 64 bare screens in a 16 × 4 grid at the defaults; the 배치 panel; and `?columns=10&gapX=0&gapY=0&marginX=0&marginY=0&phones=20&radius=24&header=1&cropTop=0` (20 mobiles edge to edge in 10 columns, rounded, with status bar and island).
- Frame timing, defaults, demo feed, the first 10 s after loading in a hidden automation tab: 57 fps, p95 18 ms, 1,487 quads. The worst frame, 233 ms, is in the loading seconds; steady state was not measured separately.
- Covering, defaults (2026-11-24, 1336 × 835, DPR 2): 64 mobiles in 16 × 4 over the whole screen, the outer columns cut by the edges; with `marginX=5&marginY=5&gapX=0&gapY=0` the margins stay clear.
- 100 mobiles (`?phones=100`, same window, demo feed at about 150 strikes/s, the last 600 frames 30 s after loading): 20 × 5, 60 fps, p95 17 ms, worst 18 ms, 2,219 quads; CPU a frame: uploads 0.36 ms, phones 0.33 ms, packing 0.59 ms, draw 0.04 ms. No atlas warning.
- The first load after a code change stayed black for about 15 s (no tile painted yet) while the dev server rebuilt the paint workers; later loads lit within the usual seconds.
- The defaults after 2026-11-25 (100 mobiles, native DPR 2.2, `sharp` 1.25, death off; 1336 × 835, demo feed, 50 s after loading): no black mobile, 60 fps, p95 17 ms, worst 18 ms, 2,341 quads, 1,337 tiles on glass, no atlas warning. x hid the menus.
- Not checked: the live feed from a screen, 한 대 and 손 with the cut glass, a narrow window, a real mobile.
