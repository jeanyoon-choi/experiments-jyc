**Z-fighting (user, 2026-11-23; the lead had reported the silver cases without seeing it).** The front of a mobile is four layers a hair apart on one plane: case, black face, screen, cover glass. Seen from far through 화면 맞춤's long lens the depth buffer could not tell them apart, and they flickered through each other. Now each layer is drawn after the one under it and pulled toward the eye by its place in the stack (`mobile/mobile.ts` `stacked`: render order and polygon offset), and the fitted view's depth range is tighter (near a tenth of the distance, far three times it). Seen clean in stills from the front and at a slant; a still cannot show a flicker, so judge it moving.

## How it is made

| Folder | What |
|---|---|
| `scene.ts` | The studio and the frame: each frame the source draws its screens and tells what each owner is doing, and the school follows. |
| `fish/` | The school, instanced, as 1's. A fish can be moved from its place (터치 지점까지). |
| `mobile/` | The slabs. The screens are one instanced mesh in the source's material. |
| `screens/source.ts` | The seam: a source gives the screens' material and per-screen attributes, is advanced once a frame, and tells where each glass is touched. Nothing else knows which source is on. |
| `screens/clips.ts` | 1's feed of clips, in a shader (화면: 클립). |
| `screens/interface/` | 2d-mobiles/2's engine (`gl/`, `paint/`, `phone/`, `apps/`, `content/`, `model/`, `objects/`) and `index.ts`, which makes a source of it. |
| `settings.ts`, `panels/` | Every option, as numbers; the panel in bubble/9's language. |

**The sheet.** The engine's compositor no longer has a canvas. It is given three.js's own WebGL2 context and draws every glass side by side onto one texture (the sheet) in its one instanced draw; each screen of the wall reads its own cell. Nothing is copied between canvases. Raw WebGL runs between three's frames: `screens/interface/index.ts` sets the state the engine relies on before, and resets three's cache after.

- The sheet's size is a budget of pixels shared by all glasses (화면 해상도, 6 million by default): 100 glasses are each 167 × 360, one glass alone 585 × 1266. Mipmaps (four levels) are made each frame for glasses seen small or at a slant.
- The engine's colours are stored and shown as they are (display values; no tone curve, no conversion).

**What changed in the copied engine** (`gl/renderer.ts`, `phone/engine.ts`, `phone/phone.ts`, `gl/shaders.ts`): no canvas, frame loop or pointer of its own; bare square glasses packed in a sheet instead of bezelled phones fitted to a window; up to 225 phones (it was 96); phones past the feed's 64 slots share slots; `Phone.hand()` tells where its glass is touched. Its own goldfish (the 2D thumb) is drawn at no opacity.

**The fish and the glass.** A source's `hand(index)` gives where glass `index` is touched and how fast the touch moves. 가장자리 잡기: the fish stays, its right fin strokes with the touch's speed. 터치 지점까지: the fish moves so that fin's tip is where the glass is touched, and goes back when the touch lets go.

## Measured (lead, 2026-11-23, MacBook Air, one visible tab, 1470 × 919 at DPR 2, pixel ratio 1.5)

10 × 10, interfaces, demo feed, steady state over the last 600 frames:

| | median | p95 | worst |
|---|---|---|---|
| 가장자리 잡기 | 16.7 ms | 17.3–19.6 ms | 17.7–26.6 ms |
| 터치 지점까지 | 16.7 ms | 17.4–18.5 ms | 18.6–21.1 ms |

- Main thread a frame: median 1.4–2.1 ms (engine: uploads 0.3, phones 0.3, packing 0.5, draw call 0.04).
- 9 draw calls for the scene, one for the sheet; about 2,300 quads.
- The first 10–15 s after loading or changing the arrangement are slower (one run: p95 30 ms) while the first tiles are painted.
- Frame times are bound to the display's 60 Hz, so they show no frames are dropped, not how much GPU is left. That is not measured.

Seen in the browser: the wall opening on interfaces; one fish with a glass sharp enough to read (notification, prices, tab bar); 터치 지점까지 moving the fish over their screens; the panel.
