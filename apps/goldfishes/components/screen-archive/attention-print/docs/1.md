# Attention

Route: `/screen/attention-print/1`, aliases `/screen/0908/attention-print`, `/0908/attention-print`. 2026-09-08.

Question: can the conflict between serial commercial language and local collective attention become legible as a changing print?

Baseline: full local copy of overlay-2d-3. Its attention, habituation, obstacle, trail-field and keyword-propagation models are retained. Mutation: a new composition and renderer palette. Cool white #f3f5f4, dense blue #172b58 and vermilion #ef3d25; Helvetica typography. Larger, more widely spaced keyword signs replace Instagram gradients. New states are solid ink, viewing states reverse into the ground, leaving states disappear. Fish and causal transmission marks share the vermilion ink. Permanent historical traces accumulate below opaque signs.

Pop-art references concern serial signs and text as image, rather than borrowed characters, comic dots or nostalgic surface effects. No mock paper texture, shadows, explanatory captions or decorative instrumentation. Only propagation visibility, trace clearing and pause remain. Clearing the visual traces does not erase the decaying behavioral memory.

Reference: [Warhol, Campbell's Soup Cans (MoMA)](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/79809). Its serial commercial signs inform the repeated keyword units; this is a compositional reference, not a claim of equivalent artistic significance.

Single 24Hz clock drives both agents and keywords; reduced motion freezes autonomous updates. Two fixed raster layers, DPR 1, 72 agents. Keyword state uses an independent session key; full reload regenerates fish and historical traces. Responsive geometry uses the same layout for DOM signs, collision boundaries, SVG propagation and Canvas fish.

Validation: static typecheck; no browser observation or claim of measured visual quality/FPS. Artistic reception remains to be assessed on the live work.
