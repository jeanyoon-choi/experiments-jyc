# 0806 temporal decay

Date: 2026-08-06 · Route: `/screen/pillars/4`

Forked from `pillars/3`. The empty field asks what changes when an active media stratum ends but leaves its historical column. A click grows one 1×1 block from the fixed present layer; when its own lifetime ends, fish attraction, playback, and growth stop together while the pillar and strata remain at that exact boundary. Click/drag selection, atlas, hard cuts, camera framing, controls, and sparse grammar remain invariant.

`Decay > block lifetime` is 0.1–10 s (default 1), measured from click rather than latest media change. Optional 2×2 companions are off by default, share the 1×1 lifetime, and never attract fish; disabling the option stops and clears only those companions. Existing instanced pillars and the 2,048-instance strata mesh freeze once per block; no frozen pillar is rebuilt per frame. The 2,048 anchor cap is an architectural workload bound, not a frame-rate measurement.

Open question: at what lifetime does a frozen trace read as ended activity rather than indefinite accumulation?
