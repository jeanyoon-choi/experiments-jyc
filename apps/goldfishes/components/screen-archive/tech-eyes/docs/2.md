# 0922 interactive

- Route: `/screen/tech-eyes/2` (legacy aliases: `/screen/tech-eyes/3`, `/screen/0922/interactive`, `/0922/interactive`)
- Date: 2026-09-28
- Baseline: [`tech-eyes/1`](./1.md), copied locally

This trial makes participant clicks the only bubble arrivals. The field starts
empty; a click on the screen creates one circle centered at that exact
position, independent of the hidden grid used to bound story identities.
The fish use the same position for attention and collision. Bubbling life,
scaling, surfaces, fish motion, and existing appearance controls inherit the
baseline. Automatic replenishment and story-to-story propagation are disabled.
The obsolete natural/target population and propagation-opacity controls are
absent. Reloading starts a new empty field.

Static checks establish the route and types. The rendered interaction and device
performance have not been observed in a browser.

## Clock-only update optimization — 2026-09-28

The story clock previously queued a full React render on every simulation step
and made the fish school rebuild its target relations even when bubble geometry
had not changed. The route now renders and rebuilds fish targets only when a
bubble's visible status, size, position, or occurrence changes. Attention still
updates bubble lifetime on the same clock; fish motion keeps its existing 24 Hz
ceiling. The pure model test, app typecheck, and scoped lint pass. The cause of
the reported regular FPS drops and any device-level improvement remain unverified
without an authorized browser measurement.
