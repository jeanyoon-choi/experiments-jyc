# clock/2-3d — bending the cube of a real 3D clock

Experimental. Created 2026-10-20. Screen `/screen/clock/2-3d` (screen only).

## Tested relation

clock/2's operation (the screen stays the flat present sweeping one span's
block; the cube itself is bent by shear, cylindrical and spherical
coordinates, constant curvature, Clifford torus, Möbius ring) on clock/1-3d's
real 3D clock: each pixel's cube point (x, y, t) after the bending map is
ray-traced as the film point (x, y) of the fixed camera at scene time t.
The clock, materials, light and camera are clock/1-3d's
([1-3d.md](./1-3d.md), `render/scene-glsl.ts`, copied).

Opens like clock/2 on spherical coordinates at 1/반지름 = −1.82.

## Observed (M4 Chrome, 2026-10-20)

- Opening (sphere −1.82, 1 h, 15:36): the red second hand curls into a spiral
  around the hub, the minute hand bends. No shadows (as clock/1-3d).
- 3D cube view: the bent block with the cut face rendered as the clock.
- 2940×1726, 60 fps (also with the cube view).
