# moving-image/3 — the cube as a continuous medium (liquid slices)

Experimental. Created 2026-10-20. Screen only: `/screen/moving-image/3` (no
phone route, no relay). Checked in desktop Chrome (M4, device-pixel-ratio 2,
canvas 2708×1406) on `https://macbook-air-5.local:2003`.

## Tested relation

Baseline: [moving-image/1](./1.md) (turn the cut plane) and
[moving-image/2](./2.md) (bend the cube). Their slices break where a slice
runs across time: frames are only cross-faded (nearest frame when flat, a
linear cross-fade switched by `fwidth` otherwise), so a moving figure combs
and doubles into strips, frame strips leave steps, the `fwidth` switch is
per 2×2 quad, the loop seam is a hard cut, shot cuts are hard edges, and the
frame border is aliased. The author's complaint: "the gaps between pixels are
not interpolated naturally, like a liquid; discontinuous points appear."

Changed variable: what lies *between* frames. Everything else is /1 + /2: the
same clips, box, slice rule (the flat plane Z = present) and bends, with the
two modes composable — the plane can be tilted (/1, pivot "now") on top of
any bend. moving-image/2's `flux` is replaced by a Möbius ring (below).

## Technique: motion-compensated time

- Offline, `scripts/build-cube-flow.sh <clip-id>` (Python + OpenCV DIS
  optical flow, Kroeger et al. 2016) writes `flow.bin.gz` + `flow.json`
  beside each clip's `clip.mp4` (which is not touched). Per frame interval i:
  forward flow F01 and backward flow F10 at quarter resolution (480×270,
  RGBA8, |s|^1.5 companding, range per clip). Flow fades to zero where
  standing still explains the next frame at least as well (black letterbox
  bars, flat or static areas DIS only fills by propagation — before this, a
  bright streak was dragged into Sintel's letterbox). Shot cuts (ffmpeg scene
  score > 0.25, or frames that differ while the flow explains almost none of
  it: fades, flashes) are listed and their flow zeroed.
- In the shader every time f between frames i and i + 1 is a picture: both
  neighbours are backward-warped to α = f − i along the flow (fixed point
  p = x − α·F01(p), two iterations each side) and blended (1 − α, α), each side
  down-weighted where its fixed point still moves (occlusion edges,
  unreliable flow). At α = 0 or 1 it is exactly the frame, so time is C0
  everywhere: no nearest-frame switch, no `fwidth` switch, no comb.
- Flat playback still shows the clip's own frames: the present snaps to the
  nearest frame while the slice spans < 0.25 frame intervals and slides to
  continuous time by 1.5 intervals. The weight is one number per screen
  (computed on the CPU from a 5×5 grid of the slice), so it adds no seam.
- Shot cuts become dissolve windows (0.6 s, never overlapping): before the cut
  the run-on is held at its last frame and fades into the run-on after it.
  Tested continuous (`cutWindows`, `cutTime`).
- The loop's last 1.5 s cross-fade into its first (period = frames − 1 − seam).
  (0.6 s first: at a 1.2 shear the band was ~40 px and read as a seam.)
- The frame's x, y border is antialiased over one screen pixel from the
  slice's own finite differences (two-sided, the smaller kept, so a glued
  seam does not darken a pixel column).
- "프레임 사이: 교차 (2번)" switches the same page back to moving-image/2's
  sampling for side-by-side comparison.

Why not the alternatives: higher-order temporal filters (cubic in t) still
blend misaligned content (ghosts, not combs); raising fps costs memory
linearly (24 fps fits only 12.5 s); only following the motion moves content
*between* frames.

## Möbius (replaces /2's "뫼비우스 플럭스", chip "뫼비우스")

The cube embedded in space as a Möbius ring (same definition as
`components/clock/2/model/geometry.mjs`, copied, not imported). D = box depth,
R_RING = 0.5 frame heights, k = 2π·R_RING/D, stretch m = 1 + a·(k − 1), ring
radius R_m = R_RING/a, a ∈ [0, 1] (a < 1e-4 = identity). Place P = (X, Y, Z) →
cube c: qx = X + R_m, θ = atan2(Z, qx), ρ = hypot(qx, Z) − R_m, t = R_m·θ/m
(|t| > t_max → nowhere, black), φ = a·π·t/D,
c = (ρ·cos φ + Y·sin φ, −ρ·sin φ + Y·cos φ, t). The t axis is bent into a
circle about an axis parallel to y at x = −R_m and the frame turns about t by
φ on the way round; at a = 1 the circle closes (θ = ±π ↔ t = ±t_max) with
the half-turn gluing (x, y, t_max) ~ (−x, −y, t_min). The flat present
Z = z_now cuts this ring obliquely: across the screen both time and twist
vary, and as the present passes the ring's middle the far side (x < −R_m)
crosses the seam into the flipped other end. Time is not wrapped here (the
ring closes it). Space is continuous across the seam (glued points); content
is not — frame(t_max) is not frame(t_min) half-turned — as at any loop seam.
At a = 1 the loop cross-fade reaches across that seam through the half-turn
(the end frames are read at (−x, −y)); for an open ring (a < 1) there is no
seam in space and the fade is the plain content loop. The solid view
raymarches the same map (bounds ±(R_RING/a + frame half-diagonal) in x, z),
from higher up; the ring reads as a twisted band.

Rejected first version (2026-10-20): the frame turned rigidly by
φ(t) = a·π·(t − t_min)/D with t = Z wrapped. On the fixed flat present that is
just one rotated frame — the author: "wrong approach — the cross-section
shows nothing interesting; it's just the same as before." (Its captures:
`captures/rejected-rigid/`.) An intermediate state also used a partial
gluing R(aπ) in the loop fade for a < 1, which drew the turned frame's
straight edges into the fade as faint polygons; removed.

Tests (node:test): a = 0 is the identity; a = 1, P = (−0.7, 0.2, ±1e-7) gives
t ≈ +t_max above and −t_max below with (x, y) negated between them (and that
is `glue(1, ·)`); on the near side a point farther round (X = −0.3 vs 0.8 at
Z = 0.3) is later; an open ring (a = 0.5) has nothing beyond its end.

Observed (Tears of Steel, playing): a = 1 — the screen is a curved, twisted
section, buildings and railings bent along the ring; as the present sweeps,
the upper-left (far side) shows the walker upside down: the flipped other end
of the clip (`ring-a1-play-*.jpg`). a = 0.5 — an open ring: a gentler bend,
and a straight black edge where the screen leaves the ring's end
(`ring-a0.5-*.jpg`). Faint red rectangles on the walker's clothes are in the
source footage (checked on the raw frames), not the medium.

## Structure

- `components/moving-image/3/` — `media/` (clip + flow loaders; flow gunzipped
  with `DecompressionStream`, unfiltered, streamed layer by layer),
  `model/geometry.ts` (+ test: bends copied from moving-image/2, tilt
  composition, Möbius ring, loop, cut windows, snap),
  `render/` (WebGL2: Y, CbCr and flow as 2D-array textures, cut-window
  table; slice and solid passes), `screen/` (copied author panel).
- Scripts: `scripts/build-cube-flow.sh` / `.py` (needs ffmpeg and a Python
  with numpy + opencv-python-headless; `PYTHON=` selects it). Built for all
  three clips: sintel 18.8 MB, bbb 8.4 MB, tears 21.9 MB gzip.

## Invariants

- Flat, the screen is the clip's own frames, sharp at 1920×1080.
- The screen rule never changes: the flat plane Z = present (tilted or not).
- Space is continuous everywhere a slice can reach; content discontinuities
  (loop seam, shot cuts) are dissolves, never steps.
- GPU memory ≤ 1.2 GB.

## Measured

- GPU memory (allocated): Y 622 MB + CbCr 311 MB + flow 156 MB = 1.09 GB for
  300 frames (+ 8 MB staging). bbb (260 frames) 0.94 GB.
- 60 fps at device-pixel-ratio 2 (2708×1406): flat, shear 1.2, a 0.5 rad
  turn, Möbius ring a = 1 with the solid view (58–60). One stretch measured
  6–17 fps for every geometry including flat — while other agents' clock
  tabs were running; it recovered to 60 when they stopped, so it was external
  load, not this page.

  "Steve Jobs iPhone 2007 Presentation (HD)" (YouTube vN4U5FqrOdQ, 1920×1080
  stream, video only, fetched with yt-dlp in a scratch venv at the user's
  request; the first 30 s were dropped because the uploader's recording shows
  a media-player bar at 15–27 s). Built with `FPS=0.33334 build-cube.sh … 870
  30` — 14.5 min at one frame per 3 s (290 frames, the GPU budget) — plus
  `build-cube-flow.sh`, plus `live.mp4` (the same span at its own 30 fps,
  `-g 15`, 188 MB) named in the manifest as `live`.
- Live window (a temporal level of detail): at one frame per 3 s the cube
  alone looked frozen at 1×. For a clip with `live`, a hidden muted video
  plays the original in step with the present (0.6 s × rate ahead; drift is
  absorbed by nudging its playback rate ±5 %, a seek only beyond 2 s × rate),
  and each presented frame is converted into a 40-slot ring (~124 MB). The
  shader reads the ring where a point's time lies inside it (sorted on the
  CPU, proportional lookup), fading to the cube over the window's last 0.15 s.
  Flat, the screen is the original at 30 fps; turned, the band near the
  present moves at 30 fps and the rest is the cube. The window follows rates
  up to 4×; beyond, the cube and its flow play alone.
- Rates: 재생 / 2배 / 4배 / 16배 / 60배 / 정지 / 역재생 (60× runs the keynote
  in ~15 s). Apple keynote
  footage: local only, ignored by `apps/xyt-cube/.gitignore`; another machine
  must rebuild it. Bent at x 0.4 the keynote lays out as bands: Jobs on stage,
  the Apple logo, slides, iPhone screens.
- The bottom-left inset is gone: a round button bottom-left pops the 3D cube
  view out to fill the screen and back (as clock/1); the "큐브 보기" chip is
  removed.
- Performance management: the canvas resolution follows the measured frame
  time (each second: slower than 19 ms steps down, at least 0.4 of the device
  ratio; two seconds at the display's rate, < 17.6 ms, steps back up). A first
  version waited for < 15 ms to step up, which a 60 Hz display never reaches,
  so a drop during loading never recovered (also in clock/*). Measured on the
  M4: the Jobs clip at full 2708×1406, 60 fps at 1×, 4× and 60×.

## Unresolved

- Occlusion edges: where a figure uncovers background the two warps disagree
  and the blend leaves a soft half-transparent fringe (Sintel's 10 fps action
  shots most; at 24 fps small). Forward-splatting with depth ordering would
  fix it at more cost.
- Fast 10 fps montage shots whose motion exceeds what DIS tracks smear into
  painterly streaks rather than combs: continuous, not faithful.
- Loop seam and shot cuts are dissolves (double images inside the band);
  whether those should stay as visible evidence of the cut is open.
- Repository weight: 54 MB of flow for four committed clips.
- Möbius ring with a tilted plane was not captured after the rewrite.
