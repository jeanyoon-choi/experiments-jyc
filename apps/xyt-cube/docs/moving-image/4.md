# moving-image/4 — time-lapse cube

Screen `/screen/moving-image/4`, `components/moving-image/4/` (a whole copy of
[moving-image/3](./3.md): same slice, bends, flow interpolation, rates and
pop-out button; only the clip list differs).

## Intent

A fixed camera makes the cube's time axis legible: every pixel stays the same
place, so a turned or bent slice shows how that place changed — hours,
evenings, decades — as layers of time beside each other instead of a cut
montage. Only time-lapses whose viewpoint never moves belong here.

## Playback

- Flat playback runs in continuous time (moving-image/3 snapped a flat slice
  to the nearest cube frame so ordinary video showed its own frames). At 4–6
  frames a second that read as stutter at 1×; here the present always lies
  between frames along the flow, so a flat time-lapse plays as smoothly as a
  turned one. Measured on the M4 (2940×1406 canvas): 60 fps steady on every
  clip (median 16.7 ms, max 17.8 ms); one ~0.1 s hitch when a clip is switched
  (texture allocation).

## Checks

- Shibuya and Brno at 6 fps / 4 fps exceed what DIS flow explains in places
  (crowds, fog); those intervals are dissolves (`cuts` in `flow.json`).
