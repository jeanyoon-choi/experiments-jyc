# cubism/2 — bending the cube, keeping the plane (note Option A)

Experimental. Created 2026-10-20. Screen `/screen/cubism/2`; phone
`/mobile/cubism/2`. Checked in desktop Chrome (M4) on
`https://macbook-air-5.local:2003` against the local fallback (the relay had
not been restarted for `cubism-2:*`); real-phone tilt is **unverified**.

## Tested relation

Source: the 1016 "Moving-Image Cube" note. A video is an (x, y, t) cube; the
screen is its xy projection. cubism/1 changed the 2D side (Option B: rotate
the cut plane). This variant changes the 3D side (Option A) and keeps the
plane exactly as ordinary playback has it: a flat present sweeping through
the clip once per duration. The question: *when the cube itself is bent,
what does the same flat plane show?* The note's Option A lines are tested one
geometry each:

| Note line | Operation | What is bent | Amount (a, b) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "x, y, t 축간의 orthogonality를 바꾸거나" | 비직교 축 `shear` | the t axis leans: t = Z + aX + bY | slopes |
| "좌표계를 구면좌표계 등으로" | 원통 좌표 `cylinder` | t becomes the angle around an axis outside the cube | bend vector in xy; curvature = its length |
| 〃 | 구면 좌표 `sphere` | frames become concentric shells around a point outside the cube | 1/radius; sign = centre behind (past) or ahead (future) |
| "리만기하학으로 올리거나" | 상수 곡률 K `curvature` | the cube sits in S³ (K > 0) or H³ (K < 0); x, y, t are its normal coordinates; the screen is the totally geodesic plane through the present | K |
| "고차원 미분다양체로" / Banpo O1 | 클리퍼드 토러스 `torus` | x and t wrapped into a Clifford torus in R⁴; a double rotation of R⁴ mixes the two circles | rotation in (e1, e3), (e2, e4) |
| "미분기하학적, '힘'적인 변환" / Banpo Flux | 뫼비우스 플럭스 `flux` | stereographic lift to S³, conformal (Möbius) boost, back | rapidity, boost direction in (t, w) |

Why these and not "any warp": each is either a coordinate system or a
geometry with no free function. Constant curvature is the only isotropic
choice (its screen obeys the curved Pythagorean theorem cos D/ρ = cos σ/ρ ·
cos d/ρ). Möbius maps are, by Liouville's theorem, the only angle-preserving
deformations of 3D space. The torus is Banpo-Xism's first link moved from
(L, B) to (x, t). All are global and smooth, and centres or axes of
curvature lie outside the cube (Banpo-Xism 0319: no local steepness). Each is
the identity at (0, 0).

Honest limit (stated in the 10/19 analysis): bending the cube and cutting it
flat equals cutting the flat cube with a curved surface. What makes this
Option A rather than B is *what the participant changes*: the cube's geometry
(shown in the solid view), while the screen's rule never moves.

## Interface

- Screen: the slice fills the screen; the solid view (bottom-left, on by
  default) shows the bent cube from the side, the past half solid, the future
  half as faint glass with its edges, and the flat screen rectangle as an
  outline. Author panel (bottom-right): geometry, clip, play/still/reverse,
  solid view, flatten. Drag or arrow keys bend; `0`/Home flattens.
- Phone: the same solid view full-screen. Press and hold, then tilt
  (left–right → a, front–back → b; one-amount geometries take both); without
  motion, drag. "평평하게" flattens the current geometry.
- Many phones: every change adds to the amount (∫ dx₁ dx₂ …, the 1015 memo);
  each geometry keeps its own amount, so switching returns to where it was
  left.

## Invariants

- At amount (0, 0) the screen is the clip itself.
- The screen plane never moves; only the cube's map changes.
- The relay owns operation, amounts, playhead, rate, clip; browsers derive
  the present, the slice and the solid. Display smoothing happens once.
- Time loops on the screen (the clip repeats along t); beyond the frame's
  x, y is black. The solid view shows the cube once, unlooped.

## Observed (desktop Chrome, 2026-10-20, Sintel trailer)

- 비직교 축: vertical bands, one shot per band — the same picture as
  cubism/1's tilt (as predicted: a lean of t is a turn of the plane).
- 원통 좌표: one side of the screen compresses many moments into thin bands,
  the other stretches one moment wide; the solid shows the top face as an arc.
  (The first version put the axis inside the frame: a singular line; fixed by
  moving it outside.)
- 구면 좌표: the centre is a disc of the present, rings outward are other
  moments — time becomes radial. (The first version put the centre inside the
  cube: as the present neared it the screen zoomed without bound; fixed.)
- 상수 곡률 K: K > 0 pinches the frame edges and rings the present with a
  band of other time; K < 0 swells the centre like a fisheye and the outer
  ring jumps shots. The two signs read as opposite.
- 클리퍼드 토러스: x and t interleave in folds; horizontal runs of the frame
  become time.
- 뫼비우스 플럭스: circular, angle-preserving rings of time and a global
  zoom; straight edges become arcs.
- 2940×1406 slice + raymarched inset: 60 fps on M4. Phones draw the solid at
  device-pixel-ratio 1 with 128 steps; real-phone cost unmeasured.

## Why spacetime/1 (10/19) failed, and what changed here

Checked in the browser on 2026-10-20: its screen is a synthetic grid/clock
clip and a press makes a lens pinch — it reads as a 2D filter, not as a
deformed moving-image cube. Causes: (1) no real footage, so nothing marked
time; (2) the cube was never visible, so no one could see that geometry, not
the picture, had changed; (3) physics metaphors (boost, mass) were chosen for
justification before perception, and the strongest visible effect was
spatial lensing; (4) 480×270. Here: 1920×1080 multi-shot footage, the note's
own Option A lines as the operations, the bent cube always visible beside
the flat plane, and the plane rule identical to playback.
