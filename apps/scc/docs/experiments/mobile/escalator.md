# Escalator

`/mobile/escalator/1` (2026-11-02) is a three.js shaft filled with running escalators, opened on a phone, at [`/mobile/escalator/[experiment]`](../../../app/mobile/escalator/[experiment]/page.tsx). Code: [`components/mobile/escalator/1`](../../../components/mobile/escalator/1/).

- Tested relation: the whole structure. A block of escalators seen from outside as one object and entered down to the step; the viewer alone moves the camera (one finger orbits, two fingers pinch-dolly and pan, the wheel dollies; 1.2–420 m from the target, no damping, no automatic motion). Only the machines move: steps and handrails.
- World: a 3 × 3 block of 28 m cells by eight storeys (H = 8 m), three banks per cell (4-lane crisscross or 2-lane), each cell at its own heading (0/90/180/270°) and each bank at its own storey phase (eighths of H); concrete columns at the cell corners. Fog (#24231f) is 0.03 inside the block and thins with the distance outside it (×30/(30 + 1.5d)) so the structure reads whole; near/far clip planes follow the same distance.
- Escalator: 30°, 1 m steps, 0.4 m pitch, C¹ transitions (radius 1 m) and 1 m flats; aluminium cleated treads/risers with yellow demarcation, yellow comb segments on the landing plates, brushed stainless skirts/decks/cladding, skirt brushes, skirt LED line, low-iron glass with Fresnel opacity and rounded newel ends, black rubber handrails that turn round the newels and scroll with the steps, terrazzo landings with glass guards, linear lamps under each slab.
- Light: PMREM of a lamp-lined room for reflections, one weak directional light, and per-vertex baked irradiance from the lamps of the same storey only (slabs above occlude the rest); steps sample the same irradiance along the step line in their vertex shader.
- Cost: all static parts are instanced per bank type × detail × material and refilled by distance to the camera whenever it moves 3 m (near detail within 22 m; far detail drops landing guards, glass ends, brushes and LEDs); steps are one instanced mesh moved entirely in the vertex shader and filled only within 38 m of the camera. 216 bank-storeys, ~700 escalators. Textures are drawn with canvas fills and gradients (the cleat normal map is a repeated 4 px tile); programs compile with `compileAsync` before the first frame. 30 Hz, no post-processing, no shadows; DPR starts at 1.0, drops ×0.8 below ~24 fps and rises by 0.1 after steady 30 Hz (0.6–1.5). Reduced motion stops the steps.
- Sound: the first touch starts a low drive rumble, 50/100/200 Hz hum and the chain clack at the step frequency.

History: the first version (same day) used a 54 m world (~920 escalators, ~23,000 steps), per-pixel canvas textures and fixed DPR 1.5; the user reported poor performance on opening and no zoom, which led to texture and count cuts, adaptive DPR and pinch zoom. The same day the automatic ride (standing on a step, riding up storey after storey, gaze settling back after a drag) was removed at the user's request — the purpose is to see the whole structure, so the camera became viewer-only with a dolly that leaves the block.

Unverified: browser check was blocked by a certificate error in the automation browser; static checks only (lint, typecheck). Frame rate, look and device thermal behaviour on a phone are unobserved.
