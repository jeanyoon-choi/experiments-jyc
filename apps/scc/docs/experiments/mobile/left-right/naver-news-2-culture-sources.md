## kbs: KBS 사장 교체

- 2026-10-20: the KBS board held final interviews with three candidates and voted to recommend 이태현 (former 콘텐츠웨이브 대표, joined KBS as a PD in 1994) to the president as the next 사장. The amended 방송법 requires three fifths of the 10 sitting directors; the exact count was not published ([경향신문](https://khan.co.kr/article/202610201843001), [기자협회보](https://journalist.or.kr/news/article.html?no=61889)).
- 이강덕 and 장한식, the two directors recommended by 국민의힘 who began their terms that day, asked to postpone the vote for lack of time to vet the candidates and walked out just before it ([경향신문](https://khan.co.kr/article/202610201843001)).
- The candidates came through a 사장후보 국민추천위원회 of 205 citizens ([경향신문](https://khan.co.kr/article/202610201843001)).
- The president approved the 인사청문요청안 on 11-06; the 과방위 floor leaders agreed on 11-10 to hold the hearing on 11-24 ([기자협회보](https://journalist.or.kr/news/article.html?no=62049)).
- 박장범, the sitting 사장, maintains his term is guaranteed to 2027-12-09. His injunction request of 08-31 at 서울남부지법 was dismissed; his constitutional complaint against 방송법 부칙 제2조 제3항 was still pending as of 10-20 ([기자협회보 11-10](https://journalist.or.kr/news/article.html?no=62049), [기자협회보 10-20](https://journalist.or.kr/news/article.html?no=61889)).
- The 편성위원회 had not met since 10-02 ([기자협회보](https://journalist.or.kr/news/article.html?no=62002)).
- "파우치" in the 강경 left cell refers to the "조그만 파우치" remark reported when 박장범 was named 사장 ([기자협회보](https://www.journalist.or.kr/m/m_article.html?no=56954)).
- Not verified: the hearing's outcome and the appointment (the hearing falls the day after this check); any party's official statement on the nomination.

## equality: 평등법 발의

- 2026-11-11: 박주민 (더불어민주당) announced the 평등에 관한 법률안 as lead sponsor at a 국회 소통관 press conference. Twelve sponsors in all: 민주당 4 (including 박주민), 조국혁신당 4, 진보당 3, 기본소득당 1 ([프레시안](https://www.pressian.com/pages/articles/2026111111525456779), [경향신문](https://khan.co.kr/article/202611090600101)).
- No criminal penalties and no punitive damages. A victim can petition 국가인권위원회; an ignored recommendation can become a 시정명령, and an ignored order draws an 이행강제금 ([서울신문](https://m.seoul.co.kr/news/newsView.php?id=20261111500138), [프레시안](https://www.pressian.com/pages/articles/2026111111525456779)).
- 국가인권위원회 recommended a comprehensive 차별금지법 in 2006; since the 법무부 notice of 2007 it has been introduced more than ten times and never reached a plenary session. Of 38 OECD members, Korea and Japan have no such law ([경향신문](https://khan.co.kr/article/202611090600101)).
- Earlier in the 22nd Assembly: bills by 손솔 (진보당, 2026-01-09) and 정춘생 (조국혁신당, 2026-02-05) ([국민참여입법센터 2215945](https://opinion.lawmaking.go.kr/gcom/nsmLmSts/out/2215945/detailRP), [2216610](https://opinion.lawmaking.go.kr/gcom/nsmLmSts/out/2216610/detailRP)).
- The UN 여성차별철폐위원회 wrote to the government on 2026-10-30 regretting the 20-year delay ([프레시안](https://www.pressian.com/pages/articles/2026111111525456779)).
- "사상 통제법" in the 극단 right cell is wording reported from opponents in early 2026 ([경향신문](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202602021600001)).
- Not verified: any reaction of 국민의힘 or of religious bodies to this particular bill, and any poll on it. The right cells therefore speak only through unnamed voices.

## jongmyo: 종묘 앞 재개발

- 세운4구역 lies across from 종묘. The revised plan raises the maximum height to 141.9 m on the 청계천 side and 98.7 m on the 종로 side; it was approved on 2026-06-18 and 세계일보 reports the 사업계획 인가 as June ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/lifestyle/sewoon-redevelopment-faces-fresh-heritage-review-after-twoyear-stalemate-near-jongmyo-shrine/12828339), [뉴시스](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20251102_0003386601)).
- 국가유산청 demands a 세계유산영향평가 and has deferred the 매장유산 review four times. 서울시 holds that the site is outside the 종묘 세계유산지구 and has no duty to be assessed. Residents say they carry over 1.4 billion won of interest a month and have sued the state for about 25 billion won ([세계일보](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20261118517139)).
- 오세훈 서울시장 called the deferrals "행정 농단" during 시정질문 at 서울시의회; the article's text gives 11-18 and its photo caption 11-17, so the code says only "이달". He also said 15 projects near 한양도성 could be affected ([세계일보](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20261118517139)).
- UNESCO's World Heritage Centre gave notice by letter that, if the assessment was not settled by March 2026, the World Heritage Committee could formally review the state of conservation of 종묘 ([뉴시스](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260511_0003625015)). 종묘 was not on the conservation agenda of the committee's July 2026 session in 부산 ([뉴시스](https://mobile.newsis.com/view/NISX20260729_0003728255), from the search summary; the page itself was not opened).
- "왕릉뷰" in the 강경 left cell is the precedent named in coverage ([다음 뉴스](https://v.daum.net/v/20251103081048100)).
- Not verified: the date and result of a fifth review.
