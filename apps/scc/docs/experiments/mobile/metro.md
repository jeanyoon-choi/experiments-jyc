# 수도권 전철 노선도

`/mobile/metro/1` (2026-11-02) at [`/mobile/metro/[experiment]`](../../../app/mobile/metro/[experiment]/page.tsx).

- Relation tested: the whole capital-area network as an official-grammar 노선도, turned 90° clockwise (west at the top, north to the right) so its long east–west extent stands in a portrait phone. Station names stay upright.
- Topology: OpenStreetMap as of 2026-11-02, all-stop route variants only (express services would add false adjacencies). 24 lines — 1–9호선, 수인분당, 신분당, 경의중앙, 경춘, 공항철도, 경강, 서해, GTX-A, 인천 1·2, 의정부, 에버라인, 우이신설, 김포골드, 신림 — 656 stations, 119 interchanges. Stations merge across lines by name within 700 m (신촌 2호선/경의중앙, 용산/신용산, 의정부/경전철의정부 stay apart, as on the official map). Every line's ends, branches (1호선 광명·서동탄·인천/신창, 2호선 성수·신정지선, 5호선 마천, 경의중앙 서울역·도라산, 경춘 광운대), and the 6호선 응암 one-way loop were checked by hand. Corrections over OSM: 용문–지평 (the 지평 shuttle lists only two stops), GTX-A 성남, and skip edges from routes missing a stop member. Pipeline: [`components/mobile/metro/extract`](../../../components/mobile/metro/extract/README.md).
- Excluded: express patterns, 교외선 (무궁화호), 인천공항 자기부상, 월미바다열차, unopened lines (위례선, 동북선, 신안산선). GTX-A is two disjoint sections (운정중앙–서울역, 수서–동탄) as OSM records on this date.
- Layout: true positions folded by component-wise asinh (north–south folds sooner); key stations (transfers, branches, ends) relax toward even spacing and 0/45/90° links. 2호선's loop then becomes a circle, its 43 stations at even angles in running order; every other key station is held on the side of the loop it really lies on (종로3가 outside, 신림 inside) while the rest relaxes again. Composition: the circle's centre is the centre of a 2.05:1 portrait frame and spans 46% of its width; beyond the circle each direction is stretched or folded on its own (exponent 0.6, so the band just outside opens and far tails fold) until the network reaches the frame edge all round. Runs of ordinary stations become octilinear paths with at most one bend; circle links are arcs. The 한강 runs through the middle of the eleven links that bridge it (7호선's 뚝섬유원지 is OSM's 자양).
- Drawing, after 서울시's 2023 서울 지하철 노선도 ([press release map, 2023-09-14](https://clik.nanet.go.kr/clikr-collection/policyinfo/77/313/2023/CLIKC8867313729797402_attach_2.pdf); [final, 2023-12-25](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2009879)): off-white ground, pale 한강 band with "한강 Han River", line colours sampled from that map's legend, line hierarchy (1–9호선 full weight; 수인분당·신분당·경의중앙·공항철도·인천1·GTX-A hollow double lines; 경춘·경강·서해 with a dashed core; 경전철 thin), ordinary stations as white dots, interchanges as the "traffic light" — one line-coloured dot per line in a dark capsule laid across the busiest link — round badges (numbers, or stacked short names) past each terminus, interchange names bold with English beneath, ordinary names regular in Korean. 서울알림체 is named first in the font stack but not bundled, so system Korean fonts render.
- Interaction: none for now. The map is fixed to the screen (no zoom or pan) so names are placed once per screen size; finger-skating interaction is planned. The first version that day had pinch/drag/double-tap zoom with names re-placed every frame, which made them flicker; removed at the user's request.
- Verification: lint, typecheck and the navigation test pass; the route answers 200 on `https://localhost:2000`. The connected browser showed a certificate warning for localhost, so the drawing was checked by running the real `draw()` through an SVG shim at 390×844, not in a live browser; on-device rendering is unverified.
- History (2026-11-02): first drawn in the older grammar (white discs ringed in line colour, near-black-ringed interchange discs, bounding-box fit); replaced by the 2023 grammar at the user's request. A first circle sized for 1.2-unit spacing crushed the 시청–을지로 arc; a centred circle without side constraints put 종로3가 inside the loop and tangled lines.
- Open: the north-east wedge (청량리–상봉–회기, 의정부 and the 1호선 tail to 연천) is still the densest part; badges pushed clear of crowding can sit far from their terminus; the 한강 is a polyline through bridge midpoints and may cross a line between bridges; whether labels should turn with the map is undecided.

## /2 — rubber sheet

`/mobile/metro/2` (2026-11-02) copies /1's map (model, layout, drawing grammar) and makes the plane deformable by finger skating, in the manner of `no-longer-human/1`'s strokes.

- Relation tested: a station dragged elsewhere — 사당 to the top of the screen, say — while the network keeps its topology and everything else, text included, bends non-Euclideanly around it.
- Touch: a touch within 26 px of a station grabs it and the station stays exactly under the finger; a touch on open map drags the space under the finger. Several fingers work at once. The deformation stays where it is left; a resize resets the map.
- Deformation: each touch is a flow along the straight path from where it went down to where the finger is, recomputed every frame from the state at touch-down. Sub-steps carry every point within R by (1 − r²/R²)² of the step (the centre by all of it) and move less than 0.1 R, far below the 0.65 R at which this kernel could fold, so each sub-step and their composition are homeomorphisms. R = max(reach, 1.2 × drag length), reach 0.34 (station) or 0.2 (open map) of the screen width, so a long drag is a scaled copy of a short one. A finger landing or lifting bakes the result as the new reference.
- Lines are sampled every 5 px; a gap stretched past 8 px gets a sample at its midpoint in the reference state and the frame is flowed again, so curves stay on the line as drawn. Every glyph is an atlas sprite with two probe points whose deformation gives the local Jacobian; badges stay upright; capsules turn with the space.
- Measured in Node on the 390×844 scene (25,271 points), same code: three successive grabs (사당 → top-left, 강남 → bottom-right, 시청 → centre) and a free stroke left 0 folded cells on a 4 px grid and no new line crossings (crossings counted only between links not already overlapping in /1's layout, where two links share a diagonal), every line gap ≤ 8 px; flow plus refinement 0.4–0.9 ms per frame on desktop. Exact Jacobian tracking for a 470 px drag: stretch ≤ 3.3×, area ≥ 0.28×.
- Useful failures, same day: (1) the first version moved a fixed-radius kernel along the finger's path, incrementally; mathematically still a homeomorphism (min det 0.03) but it plowed the map into slivers ahead of the finger — stretch 24×, area 0.03× — where thick lines merged and sampled chords crossed. (2) A divergence-free (area-preserving) flow kept area ≈ 1 but sheared 30×. (3) Catmull–Rom refinement in the deformed state added off-line samples that crossed overlapping links; replaced by reference-state midpoints.
- Budget: CPU canvas 2D. While a finger moves, frames draw at DPR 1 on their own canvas (particle budget: ≈2–3k glyph sprites, under 8,192); 140 ms after the last move one frame is drawn at device DPR (≤ 2) on a second canvas and shown instead. No frames without a finger. Per frame: one copy of the reference buffer, the flows, a refinement scan, one batched path per line (two strokes for hollow and dashed lines), one path per dot colour, ~120 capsules, one `drawImage` per glyph and badge from an immutable `ImageBitmap` atlas; no `fillText` per frame.
- Verification: lint and typecheck pass; deformation checked numerically as above. Not seen in a browser or on a device: the connected browser showed a certificate warning for localhost, and the dev server on :2000 answered 404 for every dynamic route (including untouched ones such as `/mobile/hangman/1`) when checked after its 21:37 restart.
