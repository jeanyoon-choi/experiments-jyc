# 수도권 전철 노선도

`/mobile/metro/1` (2026-11-02; elastic version since 2026-11-09) at [`/mobile/metro/[experiment]`](../../../app/mobile/metro/[experiment]/page.tsx).

- Relation tested: the whole capital-area network as an official-grammar 노선도, turned 90° clockwise (west at the top, north to the right) so its long east–west extent stands in a portrait phone. Station names stay upright.
- Topology: OpenStreetMap as of 2026-11-02, all-stop route variants only (express services would add false adjacencies). 24 lines — 1–9호선, 수인분당, 신분당, 경의중앙, 경춘, 공항철도, 경강, 서해, GTX-A, 인천 1·2, 의정부, 에버라인, 우이신설, 김포골드, 신림 — 656 stations, 119 interchanges. Stations merge across lines by name within 700 m (신촌 2호선/경의중앙, 용산/신용산, 의정부/경전철의정부 stay apart, as on the official map). Every line's ends, branches (1호선 광명·서동탄·인천/신창, 2호선 성수·신정지선, 5호선 마천, 경의중앙 서울역·도라산, 경춘 광운대), and the 6호선 응암 one-way loop were checked by hand. Corrections over OSM: 용문–지평 (the 지평 shuttle lists only two stops), GTX-A 성남, and skip edges from routes missing a stop member. Pipeline: [`components/mobile/metro/extract`](../../../components/mobile/metro/extract/README.md).
- Excluded: express patterns, 교외선 (무궁화호), 인천공항 자기부상, 월미바다열차, unopened lines (위례선, 동북선, 신안산선). GTX-A is two disjoint sections (운정중앙–서울역, 수서–동탄) as OSM records on this date.
- Layout: true positions folded by component-wise asinh (north–south folds sooner); key stations (transfers, branches, ends) relax toward even spacing and 0/45/90° links. 2호선's loop then becomes a circle, its 43 stations at even angles in running order; every other key station is held on the side of the loop it really lies on (종로3가 outside, 신림 inside) while the rest relaxes again. Composition: the circle's centre is the centre of a 2.05:1 portrait frame and spans 46% of its width; beyond the circle each direction is stretched or folded on its own (exponent 0.6, so the band just outside opens and far tails fold) until the network reaches the frame edge all round. Runs of ordinary stations become octilinear paths with at most one bend; circle links are arcs. The 한강 runs through the middle of the eleven links that bridge it (7호선's 뚝섬유원지 is OSM's 자양).
- Drawing, after 서울시's 2023 서울 지하철 노선도 ([press release map, 2023-09-14](https://clik.nanet.go.kr/clikr-collection/policyinfo/77/313/2023/CLIKC8867313729797402_attach_2.pdf); [final, 2023-12-25](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2009879)): off-white ground, pale 한강 band with "한강 Han River", line colours sampled from that map's legend, line hierarchy (1–9호선 full weight; 수인분당·신분당·경의중앙·공항철도·인천1·GTX-A hollow double lines; 경춘·경강·서해 with a dashed core; 경전철 thin), ordinary stations as white dots, interchanges as the "traffic light" — one line-coloured dot per line in a dark capsule laid across the busiest link — round badges (numbers, or stacked short names) past each terminus, interchange names bold with English beneath, ordinary names regular in Korean. 서울알림체 is named first in the font stack but not bundled, so system Korean fonts render.
- Interaction: none for now. The map is fixed to the screen (no zoom or pan) so names are placed once per screen size; finger-skating interaction is planned. The first version that day had pinch/drag/double-tap zoom with names re-placed every frame, which made them flicker; removed at the user's request.
- Verification: lint, typecheck and the navigation test pass; the route answers 200 on `https://localhost:2000`. The connected browser showed a certificate warning for localhost, so the drawing was checked by running the real `draw()` through an SVG shim at 390×844, not in a live browser; on-device rendering is unverified.
- History (2026-11-02): first drawn in the older grammar (white discs ringed in line colour, near-black-ringed interchange discs, bounding-box fit); replaced by the 2023 grammar at the user's request. A first circle sized for 1.2-unit spacing crushed the 시청–을지로 arc; a centred circle without side constraints put 종로3가 inside the loop and tangled lines.
- Open: the north-east wedge (청량리–상봉–회기, 의정부 and the 1호선 tail to 연천) is still the densest part; badges pushed clear of crowding can sit far from their terminus; the 한강 is a polyline through bridge midpoints and may cross a line between bridges; whether labels should turn with the map is undecided.

## Elastic network (now /1)

First published as `/mobile/metro/2` (2026-11-02), a copy of the static map (model, layout, drawing grammar) that lets a finger take hold of a station. On 2026-11-09 the static version was removed at the user's request and this one took its place at `/mobile/metro/1`; `/mobile/metro/2` no longer exists. The sections above describe the shared map; the static version's "Interaction: none" no longer applies.

- Relation tested: one point moved — 사당 put somewhere else — and the network answering point by point and line by line in real time, its connections unchanged. The paper does not move: the 한강, its name and every part not pulled stay put.
- Model (`physics.ts`): every station is a node; every link is a cord of nodes every 10 px, joined by distance constraints (stiffness 0.5) and bending constraints two nodes apart and through a station along one line (0.2); stations are pulled home by 0.04 per frame, cord nodes by 0.02. Position-based dynamics: Verlet step with damping 0.82, then six constraint passes; 1,441 nodes and 3,076 constraints on a 390×844 screen.
- Touch: a touch within 28 px of a station holds it under the finger; several fingers hold several stations. A released station stays exactly where it was put (held there) and the rest settles around it; grabbing it again moves it on. Touches away from stations do nothing. A resize resets the map.
- Measured response (Node, same code, 사당 dragged 250 px and held): mean displacement 163 px for stations one link away, 100 px at two, 59 px at three, 33 px at four, 13 px at six.
- Drawing: line paths through their cords' current nodes, parallel lines offset along the cord's own normals; dots on station nodes; capsules turn with the cord leaving their station; names (one atlas image each, with English for interchanges) and badges are carried by their stations. Frames run only while a finger is down or a node still moves more than 0.02 px; DPR 1 while moving, one frame at device DPR (≤ 2) 120 ms after rest.
- Observed (headless Chrome, 390×844, DPR 2, touch emulation, real route on `https://localhost:2000`): 사당 dragged to the top-left pulls 2호선 and 4호선 after it with 방배, 서초, 낙성대 following and fading; 강남 dragged to the bottom-right stretches 2호선 and 신분당선 and pulls 교대, 역삼, 신논현, 양재; the released station stays; no console errors. With 4× CPU throttling, frame intervals during the drag were 16.7 ms (median, p90 and max). Not observed on an iPhone.
- Failure, same day: the first /2 deformed the plane — a flow of the whole paper under the finger, lines and text warped with it (homeomorphic, measured to add no crossings). The user had asked for the network itself to move point by point and line by line, not the paper; replaced by the elastic network above. Along the way the plane flow showed that a fixed-radius kernel dragged along the finger's path piles the map into slivers (stretch 24×, area 0.03×), that an area-preserving flow shears 30×, and that a radius growing with the drag bounds both.
- Open: links can be pulled across each other (connections are kept, planarity is not); names keep their /1 offsets and can overlap once stations move; the river is fixed.

## /2 — the published map

`/mobile/metro/2` (2026-11-09) copies /1's elastic network and replaces /1's own layout and drawing with 서울시's published 2026-03 서울 지하철 노선도 ([news.seoul.go.kr](https://news.seoul.go.kr/culture/archives/522224); grammar after [삼서울's 2023 redesign](https://samseoul.com/branding/seoul-metro-map)). Pipeline: [`extract/official`](../../../components/mobile/metro/extract/official/README.md).

- Relation tested: the real map, not an approximation of it, made of cords: read it by pinching and dragging the paper; take hold of a station and the network answers as in /1.
- Taken from the published image (2555 × 2560; one map unit = one of its pixels, north up, square): every station's position (interchange capsules matched by line set, 59 ends and crowded interchanges read by hand, ordinary stations on the white dots along each traced line), each line's path between stations (2호선 a fitted circle, radius 525.8; elsewhere 0/45/90° runs with 14-unit rounded corners), each interchange's disc order, each name's position, size and tracking (matched in 서울알림체 against the map's ink; 639 of 655), the Seoul field and the 한강.
- Drawing as measured: ground #F7F7F7, Seoul #FFFFFF, river #DCEEFA; 1–9호선 7.0 units wide, 경의·중앙 / 신분당 / 수인분당 / 공항철도 / 인천1 / GTX-A two strokes over a 32% tint, 경춘 / 경강 / 서해 white dashes, 경전철 4.0; plain stations white dots, ringed on double and dashed lines, larger rings where a line branches; interchanges one disc per line (r 5.0) wrapped in white and a #231F20 ring, the rings of neighbours merging like an "8"; badges r 14. Colours are the official sRGB from 서울시's "노선별 색상값" PDF (GTX-A, absent there, sampled from the map).
- Type: 서울알림체 (서울특별시, 공공누리 제1유형), subset to the map's names (≈29 KB per weight). Interchange names ExtraBold ≈14.2 units with no tracking; plain names Bold ≈11.5 units with +0.1 em; English Bold ≈9.5 / Medium ≈7.0 in the map's forms (Univ., 3(sam)-ga).
- Topology differences from /1 found against the map: 경의·중앙선 ends at 임진강 (도라산 dropped); 성수지선 runs 성수–용답–신답–용두–신설동 in a "<" right of the loop.
- Quality: one canvas at device resolution (DPR ≤ 3) for every frame, moving or not; strokes are vector at any zoom; names are bitmaps at the current scale rounded up to a third of an octave (drawn at 79–100%), packed per scale in 1024² atlas pages, three scales kept, at most 6 new per frame. While the zoom changes, names reuse bitmaps of other scales and are re-rasterised 160 ms after it stops.
- Measured (frame-callback time; Chrome headless on Metal, 390 × 844, DPR 3, canvas 1170 × 2532): dragging a station 2.1 ms median, in the whole-map view 2.3 ms; at 4× CPU throttling 8.0 / 8.8 ms median with frame intervals held at 16.7 ms. Zooming at 4×: 8.6 ms median, p90 29 ms, one frame up to 80 ms when the new scale's names are rasterised. Before atlases and the zoom freeze, the same zoom cost 15 ms median / 113 ms max, and desktop Chrome spent 183 ms in drawImage over 20 frames drawing from per-name canvases. Not measured on a phone.
- Open: 16 names keep a fallback place right of their station; 49 runs on double lines fell back to even spacing where the dots could not be read; the 7089-px original could not be downloaded, so positions carry the 2555-px image's ±1 unit.

## /3: sharp elastic network

`/mobile/metro/3` (2026-11-09), a standalone copy of the elastic /1 (same model, portrait layout, physics and touch) with its rendering rebuilt for resolution and cost. Made alongside the separate /2 redesign, which it does not share code with.

- Resolution: one canvas at device DPR (≤ 3) at rest and in motion; /1 dropped to DPR 1 while moving (blurred names and lines) and swapped to DPR 2 120 ms after rest. Names and badges are rasterised once at the device scale into 2048² atlas pages and blitted 1:1 on the device pixel grid (/1: a DPR 2 atlas resampled to the screen).
- Cost: each node remembers where it was last drawn; a node that drifts more than 0.35 device px marks the 96-px device tiles its line bundles (with neighbours), dot or capsule, badge and name touch, before and after. Only those tiles are cleared and repainted, clipped, in full draw order; marks outside them are culled. At rest one exact pass catches sub-threshold drift.
- Drawing: 서울알림체 (500/700/800, subset copied from /2's font files; every name, English name and badge on this map is covered) — interchange names 800, others 500; lines are quadratic curves through cord midpoints, so stretched cords bend smoothly and octilinear corners round slightly; line weight `k·0.38` (was 0.34); interchange capsules and plain dots have minimum sizes so the traffic lights read on a phone; badges at least 7.5 px radius. Label placement is /1's greedy rule, so the narrower font changes which names fit (e.g. 청량리 now placed, 신설동 dropped).
- Measured (headless Chrome via CDP, 390×844, touch emulation, 4× CPU throttle, 사당 dragged 90 steps): script per frame median 1.6–1.8 ms vs /1 2.2–2.4 ms; repainted area median 23–26% of the device canvas at DPR 3 (/1 repainted 100% at DPR 1 = 11% of device pixels) and 26% at DPR 2 (/1: 25%). No seams after settle. Not observed on an iPhone.
- Open: as in /1, names keep their offsets and can overlap once stations move; a large multi-finger drag can approach a full-canvas repaint at DPR 3.
