# 수도권 전철 노선도

`/mobile/metro/1` (2026-11-02) at [`/mobile/metro/[experiment]`](../../../app/mobile/metro/[experiment]/page.tsx).

- Relation tested: the whole capital-area network as an official-grammar 노선도, turned 90° clockwise (west at the top, north to the right) so its long east–west extent stands in a portrait phone. Station names stay upright.
- Topology: OpenStreetMap as of 2026-11-02, all-stop route variants only (express services would add false adjacencies). 24 lines — 1–9호선, 수인분당, 신분당, 경의중앙, 경춘, 공항철도, 경강, 서해, GTX-A, 인천 1·2, 의정부, 에버라인, 우이신설, 김포골드, 신림 — 656 stations, 119 interchanges. Stations merge across lines by name within 700 m (신촌 2호선/경의중앙, 용산/신용산, 의정부/경전철의정부 stay apart, as on the official map). Every line's ends, branches (1호선 광명·서동탄·인천/신창, 2호선 성수·신정지선, 5호선 마천, 경의중앙 서울역·도라산, 경춘 광운대), and the 6호선 응암 one-way loop were checked by hand. Corrections over OSM: 용문–지평 (the 지평 shuttle lists only two stops), GTX-A 성남, and skip edges from routes missing a stop member. Pipeline: [`components/mobile/metro/extract`](../../../components/mobile/metro/extract/README.md).
- Excluded: express patterns, 교외선 (무궁화호), 인천공항 자기부상, 월미바다열차, unopened lines (위례선, 동북선, 신안산선). GTX-A is two disjoint sections (운정중앙–서울역, 수서–동탄) as OSM records on this date.
- Layout: true positions folded by component-wise asinh (north–south folds sooner), then only key stations (transfers, branches, ends) relax toward even spacing and 0/45/90° links; each run of ordinary stations becomes an octilinear path with at most one bend, evenly spaced. Shared links draw lines side by side in map order.
- Drawing: canvas; official line colours; white stations ringed in line colour, interchanges as larger near-black-ringed discs spanning parallel lines; line badges past each terminus; names placed greedily (interchanges first) to clear names, stations and badges and cross the least line; English under Korean when zoomed in. Pinch/drag/wheel, double-tap zooms ×2.4 (at maximum, back to the fit).
- Verification: lint, typecheck and the navigation test pass; the route answers 200 on `https://localhost:2000`. The connected browser showed a certificate warning for localhost, so the drawing was checked by running the real `draw()` through an SVG shim at 390×844 (fit, ×2 and ×4 zoom), not in a live browser; touch feel and on-device performance are unverified.
- Open: aspect relies on hard north–south folding, which compresses northern Seoul; whether labels should turn with the map is undecided.
