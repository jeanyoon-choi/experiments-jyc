# 정치인의 거짓말 (입만 열면 거짓말) — /mobile/face-trace/politician-lie/1

2026-11-16. Experimental. A subfamily of [face trace](face-trace.md). The front camera is a mirror enlarged onto the participant's lips; whenever the participant's mouth opens, a famous filmed lie plays exactly in the opening between their lips, picture and sound, and stops when the mouth shuts; the next opening is the next lie. The camera and MediaPipe Face Landmarker run locally; nothing is saved or sent. Camera access starts on a tap. Hiding or leaving the page stops the tracks, and returning restarts them once access has been granted.

## Contract

- Mirror, enlarged on the lips (`zoomFrame` in `mouth.ts`): the whole screen is the mirrored camera, untouched outside the opening, enlarged so the mouth is about 0.72 of the canvas's short side and held at the screen centre. Mouth width is taken as 0.55 of the distance between the outer eye corners (landmarks 33, 263), which does not change as the lips move. The view eases after the mouth (120 ms for position, 260 ms for size), is at most 8× the plain cover, never shows past the camera frame's edge, stays put while the face is lost, and is the plain cover until a face is first found. The outline is measured in the same view the frame is drawn in, so it stays on the lips while the view moves. The camera requests 1920×1080 for this enlargement.
- Opening ([`mouth.ts`](../../../components/mobile/face-trace/politician-lie/1/mouth.ts)): the inner-lip ring (20 landmarks, 78 → 308 along the upper lip and back along the lower), with a Catmull-Rom midpoint between each pair, makes a 40-point closed outline in canvas pixels. Still lips are steadied against landmark noise; moving lips pass through unfiltered so the outline never trails the mouth. Landmarks are read from a 640-wide copy of the same frame that is drawn.
- Clip in the opening ([`renderer.ts`](../../../components/mobile/face-trace/politician-lie/1/renderer.ts)): one full-screen WebGL2 pass. Inside the outline (0.9 px feather) the clip replaces the camera, upright along the mouth's own axis and darkened to 45% just behind the lips. The clip is as wide as the opening until the opening is taller than the clip's proportions allow, and the clip row at the lie's `focus` (0.6 of its height, between the speaker's nose and mouth; 0.75 for one clip) sits at the mouth centre: a barely open mouth shows the speaker's lips, a wide one the whole face. Only pixels inside the mouth's bounding box walk the outline.
- Open and shut: opening is the inner-lip gap (landmarks 13–14) over mouth width (61–291), as in `face-trace/1`. The clip fades in from 0.03 to 0.065. It plays above 0.06 and pauses once the ratio has stayed under 0.035 for 180 ms, so the lip closures inside speech do not stop it. When the mouth shuts the reel is moved to the start of the next lie and waits there, so each opening begins a new lie; a mouth held open runs on through the reel, which loops. A lost face keeps the last opening for 400 ms.
- Sound ([`sound.ts`](../../../components/mobile/face-trace/politician-lie/1/sound.ts)), two ways, one at a time. Web Audio: the reel's sound alone (`lies.m4a`) is decoded once; the audio context is created and resumed inside the camera tap with one silent sample, any later tap resumes it, and on iOS the audio session is set to `playback`. While that context is running with the sound decoded, the video is muted and each frame the sound starts at the picture's current time, stops when the clip pauses, and is lined up again if the two drift more than 0.25 s apart. Whenever it is not, the video's own sound is let through instead; the video is started unmuted inside the same tap for that.
- Loudness follows the mouth: level is 0 at the opening ratio where the clip begins to show (0.03) and rises linearly to full at 0.4, eased over 40 ms; it applies to both ways (Web Audio gain, or the video's `volume`, which iOS ignores, so there the video's own sound is only on or off). Nothing of the clip is heard before a mouth shows it: the video is started muted inside the tap.
- Open issue, 2026-11-16: on the participant's iPhone in Safari neither the first version (video element alone) nor the Web Audio version gave any sound; the cause is not known and the two-way version above has not been tried there. Adding `?sound` to the address shows a two-line readout (context state, decoded or not, voice on or off, audio session type; video playing, muted or own sound, any refusal, mouth ratio) for reading the state on a device with no console.
- Reel ([`lies.ts`](../../../components/mobile/face-trace/politician-lie/1/lies.ts)): twelve lies joined into `public/video/face-trace/politician-lie/lies.mp4` (79.1 s, 320×240, 25 fps, H.264 with a keyframe at every lie's start, mono AAC 64 kb/s; 1.5 MB) and its sound alone, `lies.m4a` (0.66 MB). One video element and one decoded sound serve all of them; `lies.ts` holds each lie's start and end. Every clip is cropped to 4:3 around the speaker's face with the mouth near the frame's middle, and levelled to about −19 dB mean with a limiter. See the ledger below. `clinton-1998-01-26.mp4` in the same folder is the first version's clip and is no longer loaded.
- Budget: landmarks run on every new camera frame the device can afford (at most the camera's 30 per second, fewer when inference is slow); the canvas draws at up to 2× device pixels.

## Evidence

2026-11-16. `pnpm typecheck` and eslint pass. Viewed in the browser at the HTTPS origin, 1022×639 at 2× (2044×1278 canvas), with portraits from `public/images/face-voronoi/portraits/` fed in through a canvas stream as a stand-in camera.

- Closed mouth (`030.jpg`): the screen holds the lips, with the nose tip above and the chin below; clip paused, no signal at the audio output.
- Open mouth (`031.jpg`): the lips fill the screen and Clinton's whole face sits inside the opening, edges following the lips, shaded behind them. After a real click on 카메라 켜기 the audio context was running and an analyser on its output read up to 0.24 RMS while the mouth was open and 0 while shut; closing paused the clip (held at 3.24 s) and reopening resumed picture and sound from there. No console errors.
- Frame timing with the mouth open, 180 frames: median 16.7 ms, p95 17.6 ms, max 34 ms (desktop), the same as before the enlargement.
- The enlarged skin is soft: the stand-in is a 720-pixel portrait and a live mouth is enlarged about threefold from the camera frame.
- After the two-way change the same run read 0.24 RMS open and 0 shut again, with the readout showing `context running · buffer 14.2 · voice on` and the video muted.
- Loudness: from the tap until the first open mouth the output read 0 and the video was never unmuted. With the open-mouth stand-in squashed vertically to change its opening, over the same stretch of the clip: ratio 0.27 → level 0.65, mean 0.056 RMS; 0.35 → 0.86, 0.083; 0.43 → 1.00, 0.088; shut → 0.
- Unverified: sound on an iPhone (see the open issue), sound heard from a speaker (only the signal was measured), a live speaking mouth, how steady the enlarged view is with a moving head, sound and frame cost on a mobile, and whether the 180 ms hold feels right in real speech.
