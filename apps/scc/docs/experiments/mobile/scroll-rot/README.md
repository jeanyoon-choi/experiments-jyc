# Mobile scroll-rot

Organs worn down by the scrolling feed. Each organ is its own subfamily, `scroll-rot/<organ>/<n>`, and every route is swiped like reels: one reel further, one level further gone. The family began as `mobile/brain` on 2026-11-09 and was renamed on 2026-11-16 so that more organs can join the brain; the eye followed the same day. Each route is independent; none imports another.

- [brain/1](brain-1.md): a hyper-real MRI brain, wet and turning on black, rotting further in spreading patches with every reel; swiped on iOS's paging spring beside the Instagram action rail; every tenth swipe lands on Zuckerberg's face. `/mobile/scroll-rot/brain/1`.
- [brain/2](brain-2.md): brain/1 turned by the viewer's own head: the front camera reads yaw, nod and tilt and the brain follows as a mirror would; without a camera it is brain/1. `/mobile/scroll-rot/brain/2`.
- [eye/1](eye-1.md): one eyeball looking about; with every reel its white reddens and yellows, its cornea clouds, and rot spreads in patches; every tenth swipe lands on Zuckerberg's face. `/mobile/scroll-rot/eye/1`.
- [eye/2](eye-2.md): eye/1 turned by the viewer's own head, as brain/2 is: the front camera reads yaw, nod and tilt and the eye follows as a mirror would; without a camera it is eye/1. `/mobile/scroll-rot/eye/2`.
