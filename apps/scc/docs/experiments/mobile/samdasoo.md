# 삼다수

`/mobile/samdasoo/1` (2026-11-02) sets one bare 500 mL 제주삼다수 bottle, without its label, on a studio floor in three.js, at [`/mobile/samdasoo/[experiment]`](../../../app/mobile/samdasoo/[experiment]/page.tsx).

- Geometry (cm, `scene.ts`): lathed PET shell 6.6 cm across and 21.6 cm to the cap; five-foot petaloid base, seven grip grooves (four below, three above the recessed, empty label panel), a cosine shoulder to a 1.36 cm neck with its support ring. Cap: one 96-ridge knurled lathe including its tamper band.
- Shading: no lights, environment or tone mapping. Every surface is a function of its view angle only (`ShaderMaterial`): water pale `#d8edf5` at the facing centre to `#4f9cc6` at the turning edge with one soft fixed band of light; PET only as edge ink `#5d7a8c`, so grooves, shoulder and neck draw as lines; cap flat `#2f8fd0` stepped by one fixed direction. Flat `#eef1f2` ground and one soft contact shadow.
- Interaction: slow auto-turn; one-finger drag orbits (polar limited), pinch zooms; auto-turn resumes 2.5 s after release. DPR capped at 2.
- Phone motion (2026-11-09): a tap (not a drag) on the screen asks for the orientation sensor (`DeviceOrientationEvent.requestPermission` where present). Once granted, the phone's turn since that moment, taken in screen axes (corrected for `screen.orientation.angle`), is given to the bottle in view axes about its middle, so the bottle turns exactly as the phone does; auto-turn stops and the floor shadow hides. Drag still orbits.
- Cap and pouring (2026-11-09): a tap on the cap (ray hit, or within 40 px of its centre) unscrews it — 1.5 turns rising 0.7 cm over 0.75 s — then it springs off along the neck and tumbles out of view; it does not come back until reload. The water is one fixed volume (cm³, starting at the old fill line): each frame the level that holds it is found from ~5,000 equal-volume sample points of the inside, the water lathe (the whole inside to the mouth) is discarded above that world-level plane, and the surface is the plane cut to the inside, rebuilt as a 72-point fan. With the cap off, once the level is above the lowest point of the mouth's rim, water leaves at `min(140, 32·√depth)` cm³/s; the stream is one opaque tube through up to 256 points (one per frame) flying ballistic arcs at 980 cm/s², thinning with speed, broken where pouring stops. Instanced drops were tried first and read as beads/scales.

History (all 2026-11-02): the first version wrapped a drawn Hallasan label and used physical materials (transmissive water, additive PET reflections under RoomEnvironment, a key light, a floor caustic). The label, key light and caustic were removed at the user's request (bottle only, light too strong); the remaining physical render was then judged tacky (milky body, glowing white halo at neck and edges, stock-render look) and replaced by the view-angle shading above.

Checks: lint and typecheck; seen in the browser at a desktop window size (front view and after a drag orbit). Not yet seen on a phone. Phone motion checked on desktop with synthetic `deviceorientation` events (permission stubbed): beta 90→55 tips the top away, alpha +30 turns the bottle 30° counter-clockwise. Cap tap, level surface at 60°, pouring at 125° and emptying at 175° checked the same way; frame time while pouring 16.7 ms mean, 17.7 ms max (desktop).
