# 전광판

`/mobile/led-banner/1` (2026-11-16) is the last screen of a 전광판 app (LED banner, LED scroller): the black screen with huge lettering that is held up at a concert, a graduation or an arrivals gate. It lives at [`/mobile/led-banner/[experiment]`](../../../app/mobile/led-banner/[experiment]/page.tsx). Only that final screen is built; the editor in front of it (text field, colours, speed) is left out.

- Reference: App Store screenshots of [모두의응원 LED 전광판](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id6737309240), [전광판어플](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id6737825691) and [울트라텍스트](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id884075159), read on 2026-11-16. Taken from them: a black ground, the handset's own bold gothic, lettering read with the mobile turned (heads of the letters to the right in portrait), two fitted lines or one running line, and the looks 네온, 픽셀 LED, plain, 플래시 on yellow, and rainbow. Their emoji are not used.
- Signs (`banners.ts`): six messages in the grammar of cheering (축하해, 고마워요, a chant) whose object is the competition for jobs, written by the user, each with one look. 너가 떨어져서 내가 붙었어! (pink neon, still), 탈락 축하해! 가시밭길만 걷자 (LED lamps, running), 오늘도 실수해줘서 정말 고마워요 (mint, still), 불합격해 (black on yellow, inverting 1.25 times a second), 실수해줘서 고마워 (rainbow, running), 불합! 탈락! 불합! (blue neon, running). A tap shows the next one. The first build the same day carried plain cheering messages (우리의 자랑이에요!, 졸업 축하해! 꽃길만 걷자, 사랑해 and three more); the user replaced them and that set is not kept.
- Lettering (`glyphs.ts`): each sign is drawn once into a canvas sheet. Red holds the letters with a thin soft edge, green a near halo, blue a wide one (canvas shadow blur, so it also works where `ctx.filter` does not). A still sign is fitted to the screen's proportions on a 2048 px sheet; a running one is a 384 px strip.
- Light (`shader.ts`): one full-screen WebGL2 fragment shader. The red channel is cut sharp again at screen resolution, so a small sheet stays crisp on a 3× display. Neon is a tube: tint at the edge, whiter along the middle of each stroke, a wide tinted glow, and a 60 Hz hum of 3.5 %. The LED look samples the sheet once per lamp on 34 rows, draws each lamp as a disc with a brighter centre, leaves unlit lamps faintly visible and moves the text one whole lamp at a time. A running sign moves at 1.15 band heights a second.
- The page asks for a screen wake lock on first touch and when it becomes visible again, as a sign held up must not dim.

Checks (2026-11-16): lint, typecheck and the navigation registry test pass, and the route answers 200 with the canvas in its HTML. The automation browser did not trust the local certificate, so the route itself was not opened. The same sheet and shader code was run in a browser tab at 390 × 844 (2×) and compared with the references; from that, strokes on the sheet were dropped (they closed the counters of 늘, 줘, 콜) and the LED weight and rows were adjusted until the Hangul read. Shader cost there: 0.11–0.27 ms per frame at 1170 × 2532 on a desktop GPU. Not yet seen or timed on a mobile; the fallback font and weight on Android are unchecked. The browser comparison was made with the first cheering messages; the current two still signs have seven and eight glyphs on their widest line, so their lettering is smaller, unseen.
