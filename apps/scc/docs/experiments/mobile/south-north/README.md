# Mobile south-north

Experiments on the relation of the South and the North, to be widened with further interactions of the same kind. The family was `up-down/` until 2026-11-23, when the folders, routes (`/mobile/up-down/…` no longer exists), assets and records were renamed. So far these are experiments where the mobile's front–back tilt is the peninsula: tipped up, the screen brings out the North; tipped down, the South. Each interface is its own subfamily, `south-north/<interface>/<n>`, as in [left-right](../left-right/README.md). The family began on 2026-11-16.

- [tv-news/1](tv-news.md): the main evening news of both sides as one broadcast; the anchor, her studio and her voice morph between MBC 뉴스데스크 and 조선중앙TV 보도 with the tilt. `/mobile/south-north/tv-news/1`.
- [tv-news/2](tv-news.md#2-marks-in-the-corners): /1 with a minimum of guidance: a flag in the lower left that mixes the 태극기 and the 인공기 by the picture's share, and an information button in the lower right. `/mobile/south-north/tv-news/2`.
- [tv-news/3](tv-news.md#3-more-pairs-under-the-anchors): /2 with nine more pairs under the anchors, one swipe each: parades, state films, concert stages, streets, towers, beaches, noodles, dances, missiles. `/mobile/south-north/tv-news/3`.
