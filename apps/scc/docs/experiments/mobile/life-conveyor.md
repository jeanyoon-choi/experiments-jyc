# 인생 컨베이어

`/mobile/life-conveyor/1` (2026-11-09) is a virtual population riding a hundred years on ten conveyor belts, at [`/mobile/life-conveyor/[experiment]`](../../../app/mobile/life-conveyor/[experiment]/page.tsx). Code: [`components/mobile/life-conveyor/1`](../../../components/mobile/life-conveyor/1/). It criticises the fixed Korean life course: everyone is told the same thing at the same place on the belt, prompted by 허준이's 2022 서울대 졸업 축사 (취업, 창업, 결혼, 육아, 교육, 승진, 은퇴, 노후 준비, ending in a hospital single room).

- Layout: ten rows in decimal order — the bottom row is ages 0–9 left to right, the next 10–19, up to the top row 90–100 — so the bottom-left corner is age 0 and the top-right corner is 100. Each row is a thin stadium belt whose cleats travel right at the figures' speed; a person reaching x9.99 reappears at the left of the row above.
- Figure: the 졸라맨 of [finger-network/3](finger-network-3.md) (rest endpoints and joint construction copied into `model/figure.ts`), white 1.5 px strokes on black. Aged by curves only: height 0.30 of adult at birth → 1.0 at 18 → 0.92 at 100; head 2.2× the adult proportion at birth → 1× at 18; stoop forward from 60 to 100. Each person has its own sex, stature (0.93–1.06) and arm spread (0.85–1.15).
- Couples: a willing single (85 %) aged 26–40 marries the nearest-aged willing single of the other sex within 5 years (hazard 0.8/yr). A couple rides at the mean of their two ages, husband left, wife right, inner hands joined; positions ease (rate 8/s) so the pair closes in after the wedding and separates when widowed.
- Births come only from couples: wife 25–42, at most 4 children, 1.2 years apart, hazard 0.35/yr × (110 / head count)², clamped 0.1–8, so the head count holds near 110–140 (checked over ten lifespans in `life.test.mjs`). The newborn leaves its parents' joined hands and arcs in 1 s to age 0 at the bottom left; its age waits until it lands.
- Lines (`model/script.ts`): about thirty fixed-age cues from 돌잡이 through 의대반, 수능, 입대 (men), 취업/공시, 결혼, 전세대출, 육아휴직, 청약, 승진, 희망퇴직, 치킨집 창업·폐업, 은퇴, 국민연금, 요양병원 to 1인실 대기; some depend on marriage or children ("결혼은 언제 하니?", "애는 언제 낳니?"). The mother says "아들입니다"/"딸입니다", a spouse left behind "사별", a death at 60+ "대학병원 1인실, 준비 완료", earlier "아직 대출이 남았는데", and reaching 100 "준비만 하다 끝났네". Each person's cue ages shift by up to ±0.4 years.
- Bubbles: up to one per 40,000 px² of screen; a line is dropped when its bubble would overlap another. Rounded box above the head to the right with a tail to the head, 11 px system Korean sans, white on black; it follows the speaker for 2.6 s and fades, staying where its speaker fell.
- Model: 1 simulated year per second; annual hazard 0.0025 under age 1, then 0.00015 + 0.00002·e^(0.1·age) (life expectancy ≈ 82). A death or reaching 100 tips the figure off the belt: it steps forward, falls a row and turns while fading over 1.1 s. The page opens after two simulated lifespans of burn-in. No interaction.
- Cost: one canvas 2D path for all living figures, one per falling figure and bubble, two for the belts; DPR ≤ 1.5. Measured 16.7 ms median / 17.7 ms max frame (desktop Chrome, ~120 people).

History: the first version (same day) seeded births by a Poisson clock with no couples or lines; the user asked that children come from hand-holding couples and that each life point speak its scripted event.

Verified 2026-11-09 in the browser on a 390 × 639 field (the automation window could not be resized to a phone); not yet seen on a phone.
