# `/bubble/2` — `/1` with its network and visual parameters open (2026-10-08)

- **Baseline:** a standalone copy of [`/1`](1.md) (7-glsl-6): route 7's adaptive PageRank web, the soap-bubble raft
  renderer and layout, unchanged.
- **Changed:** the controls only. Two round triggers at the bottom right, in the grammar of
  `/transportation/road-signs/direction/4`:
  - **Network** — the web's own parameters: 네트워크/순위 view, quality volatility, rank concentration (appeal floor),
    new pages per second, the probability d of following a link, and the page count (restarts the web).
  - **Visual** — the renderer's main constants, opened as uniforms, plus the raft's scale and flow: bubble area
    (share of the screen), threshold for drawn flow portions, film thickness, interference colour, film current speed,
    swirl, dome depth, grazing-angle reflection, wall brightness, reflection strength (was window reflection), ambient, exposure. Defaults are
    `/1`'s values exactly; "bubble/1 값으로" restores them.
  - **Reflected surroundings** (visual panel, added 2026-10-08): 창 (`/1`'s window, the default), 흐린 하늘, 스튜디오,
    밤 도시, 나뭇잎 빛, 물빛, or an uploaded image wrapped round as a panorama. Every mode except 창 reflects a direction
    computed from the film normal, weighted by Fresnel and by 반사 세기.
  - **Palette:** colour pickers for 빛 (light reflected and lighting the film), 막 (film tint) and 배경 (what lies
    behind; it shows faintly through the films), and presets 무채 (`/1`), 새벽, 호박, 이끼, 종이. White, white and black
    reproduce `/1`.
  - **Reflection options** (all 0 by default, which is `/1`): 반사 곡면 blends `/1`'s shallow cap into a full sphere
    over each bubble's disc (held to the cap within a few px of a wall), so one bubble holds the whole surroundings
    like a fisheye; 환경 선명도·대비 narrows the surroundings' soft edges, widens their range and rolls highlights off;
    반사에 간섭색 입히기 colours the reflection by the film's interference instead of laying it over the film;
    뒷면 반사 adds the far inside face mirroring the scene upside down. Uploaded images are sampled with seam-free,
    sharpened derivatives. Studio, sphere 1, contrast 0.8: 6.4 / 15 ms, 60 fps (DPR 2, 1470 × 706).
- **Purpose:** to move back and forth between network behaviour and visual parameters and find, by adjustment, what a
  better bubble rendering of an evolving network needs — instead of rebuilding the substrate.

## Goldfish (2026-10-09)

- **Changed:** a third trigger (금붕어) adds an adaptive goldfish school around the raft. 250 fish is the default
  (user, 2026-10-11; it began at 0); at count 0 the route is `/1` unchanged, and the school, its grid and its
  three.js layer exist only once fish are asked for (the renderer by dynamic import).
- **Fish:** the 3D goldfish of Goldfishes [`/screen/tech-eyes/1`](../../../../../goldfishes/components/screen-archive/tech-eyes/docs/1.md)
  — its body, fins, eyes, materials, lights, palettes, top-locked orthographic camera, pixel ratio 1 and current
  size ×0.70 — copied into `goldfish-scene.ts` without traces, target lines and rings (apps share no imports).
  The school (`goldfish.ts`) keeps that route's attention terms: novelty, habituation, crowding, schooling and the
  orientation-aware no-overlap rule.
- **Behaviour:** a fish is a surfer of the web. When its attention lapses (주의 지속, or earlier when bored) it follows
  one of its page's links with probability 링크를 따라 헤엄칠 확률, in proportion to the link weight — PageRank's
  random surfer — or picks by salience: displayed rank, novelty of new pages, its own habituation and the crowd. It
  reaches the exposed part of its page's free face and patrols back and forth along it (가장자리 오가기), turning
  where a neighbour closes the arc. A hold point counts only with open water 36, 80 and 130 px beyond it, so pages
  buried in the raft and cavities between its bubbles are never goals.
- **Never inside a bubble:** every frame each fish is resolved against exactly the discs the foam draws (pages and
  travelling portions): radial projection, then the crossing of two exclusion circles in a notch, then a ring search
  when wedged by three or more or held at the field edge. A fish found enclosed by bubbles on two checks running
  (12 rays, 220 px), and every fish when the web restarts, swims in again from the field's edge.
- **Performative:** contact time per page, scaled by 품질에 주는 영향 (default 0.2, gain 0.25) and divided by the
  school (at least 30 fish), raises that page's log quality (`attend`, at most 1.5; it reverts at 0.08/s as before).
  At 0.4 and a cap of 3 the first browser look showed the raft collapsing into a few giants within 20 s, so both
  were lowered; 0 makes the fish pure observers.
- **Controls:** 수 (0–400), 색 (tech-eyes/1's six, plus 모노크롬: body #8a8a8a, fins #c4c4c4), 크기, 속도, 링크를 따라 헤엄칠 확률, 새 페이지에 끌림, 싫증, 주의 지속, 무리 짓기,
  가장자리 오가기, 품질에 주는 영향; 기본값으로 restores all but the count.

## Groups (2026-10-09)

- **Changed:** 그룹 (network panel) 1–4, default 1. Initial links, discovered candidates and new pages' links stay
  inside a group, so the web is n disconnected parts (echo chambers). Only PageRank's random jump and a hand-drawn
  link cross between them; a page made by hand joins the group it is dragged from or the group placed nearest.
  Each group's gravity pulls toward its own place (two side by side, three in a triangle, four in a square;
  transposed on a portrait screen). Changing it starts the web over.
- **Diverse groups (same day, after "no diversity between groups — even the sizes are equal"):** each group has a
  profile around the panel's parameters (`groupProfiles`): its share of the pages and of new pages, a shift of its
  mean log quality, and multipliers on the appeal floor (hub-dominated ↔ even), quality volatility, adaptation and
  discovery. Every trait takes evenly spaced values dealt in a seeded order, so groups always differ, in different
  ways. 그룹 다양성 (0–1, default 0.6; 0 = alike) sets the spread; 다시 섞기 draws another seed. With 0.6 and four
  groups the browser showed 47 / 23 / 16 / 33 pages after 100 s, one raft held by a single giant, another with
  several mid-sized pages round a bed of small ones. A group's quality reverts to its own mean, and fish
  attention is capped relative to it.
- **Invariant:** with one group the profile is neutral and the model consumes the same random numbers and produces
  the same links and ranks as before (checked against the committed model over 900 steps); the layout call is
  unchanged.

## Checks (2026-10-09)

- Pure tests (`goldfish.test.ts`, `model.test.ts`): over 40 s with a swelling page and portions crossing open water no
  fish came closer to any disc than its side reach; buried pages are never targeted; a fish shut inside a ring of
  bubbles leaves it; groups never link automatically, differ in size and character (alike at diversity 0), and one
  group equals the original web.
- Node, one school step: 0.06 / 0.18 ms median / p95 (60 fish, 100 pages), 0.43 / 0.65 ms (400 fish),
  1.9 / 4.1 ms (400 fish, 1,000 pages, 1,500 portions).
- Browser (HTTPS dev server, hidden tab driven by a MessageChannel shim, 1470 × 706, foam at DPR 2, GPU synchronised
  by a 1-pixel `readPixels`; the machine was slower than the README's measurement day): `/1` 12.9 / 16.6 ms;
  `/2` no fish 11.9 / 15.8; 100 fish 14.7 / 19.0; 400 fish 11.1 / 19.5. Script time with 400 fish 2.1 ms median
  against 1.0 ms with none. Differences between fish counts are within run-to-run noise except the p95.
- Seen in the browser: fish render as tech-eyes/1's fish and stay on the outside of the raft; three groups float
  apart with their own fish; after switching 4 → 1 groups the school re-entered and settled round the new raft.
